<DESCRIPTION>
Keep the direct-demand reward, but add a restrained quadratic reward for completing two-hop routes. Unlike a fixed reward for every two-hop path, divide each demand’s contribution among its possible intermediaries and discount intermediaries with high total in/out demand. This limits overcounting and makes transit capacity a consideration, while preserving the baseline as the dominant signal. The number of quadratic terms is at most n(n−1)(n−2), within the stated limit.
