<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the two-hop reward budget from 0.62 to 0.56, one of the suggested sweep values. The current setting improved median gaps but coincided with worse best gaps and a lower feasible rate than the earlier program. Slightly reducing indirect rewards preserves the existing path support and allocation while giving direct-link demand a stronger relative influence; this is a targeted test of whether that balance also improves the best sampled topologies and feasibility.
</DESCRIPTION>
