stronger_edge_discount
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the discount on two-hop paths that use edges with substantial direct demand, changing only the edge-load factor and leaving relay-node pressure unchanged. This makes indirect-path rewards favor combinations of less-loaded edges, which may reduce competition for the same links and improve load balance. The change is deliberately isolated so its effect can be evaluated against the current program.
</DESCRIPTION>
