<DESCRIPTION>
Strengthen the two-hop signal while discounting paths through heavily loaded transit nodes and paths whose edges already have substantial direct demand. Keep the positive-demand OD gate so zero-demand pairs do not generate quadratic terms. This favors short paths that can plausibly serve meaningful traffic without concentrating too much load on busy nodes or edges; the coefficient is increased from 0.06 to 0.15 to make the path signal more influential alongside direct-link rewards.
