cap_twohop_reward
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Cap the combined two-hop reward for each positive-demand origin-destination pair at 65% of its direct-link reward. This keeps the existing demand-sensitive ranking among intermediate-node choices, while preventing the many possible two-hop paths for one OD pair from collectively overwhelming the direct-link signal. It also limits the influence of indirect rewards on typical sampled topologies.
</DESCRIPTION>
