<DESCRIPTION>
Lower the relay-pressure floor from 0.75 to 0.65 as an isolated calibration test. This gives relatively low-pressure nodes a little more two-hop path reward, making it more attractive to use relays that appear to have spare capacity. The current floor is 0.75, so this change directly tests the suggested lower-floor variant without altering the demand statistics or edge-load discount.
</DESCRIPTION>
