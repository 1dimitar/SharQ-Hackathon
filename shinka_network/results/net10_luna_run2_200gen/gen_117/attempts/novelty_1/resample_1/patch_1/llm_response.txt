tighten_detour_cap
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the aggregate two-hop reward per origin-destination pair from 0.75 to 0.5 times its direct-demand reward. The current cap still gives detours a substantial combined incentive across many possible intermediates; tightening it should help preserve the direct-link signal and make low-energy topologies less dependent on diffuse two-hop rewards. The existing per-pair scaling continues to preserve transit-node preferences, while this change adds no terms or runtime.
</DESCRIPTION>
