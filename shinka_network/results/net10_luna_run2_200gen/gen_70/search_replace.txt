<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the mild concave component in both direct-link and two-hop demand signals. This gives medium-demand OD pairs more influence relative to the largest demands, which may encourage a more balanced topology and improve typical sampled solutions. The adjustment keeps direct demand dominant and does not add quadratic terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
