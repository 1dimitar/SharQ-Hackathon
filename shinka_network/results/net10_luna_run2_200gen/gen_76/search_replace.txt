<DESCRIPTION>
Use a restrained blend of linear and square-root normalized demand for two-hop rewards, while leaving direct-link rewards unchanged. The square-root component gives moderate-demand OD pairs more influence relative to the largest demands, which may help the selected links provide useful indirect routes for a broader range of traffic. The blend remains bounded and preserves the existing pressure and edge-load discounts.
</DESCRIPTION>
