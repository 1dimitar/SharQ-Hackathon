cap_indirect_budget
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Cap each OD pair’s total two-hop reward relative to its direct-link signal, while keeping the existing evidence-based allocation among intermediate nodes. The current budget is proportional to demand but can still add substantial incentive for a pair even when the direct-link signal is already strong. Limiting it to 38% of the direct signal reduces the chance that several indirect path terms collectively overwhelm direct-link preferences, without removing path-specific guidance.
</DESCRIPTION>
