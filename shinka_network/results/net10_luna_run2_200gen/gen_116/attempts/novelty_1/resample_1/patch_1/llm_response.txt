route_quality_clamp

<DESCRIPTION>
Clamp each OD pair’s candidate two-hop route scores to 0.8–1.4 times their mean before allocating its indirect reward. This preserves route preferences while preventing a few intermediates from dominating the reward. The narrower range may make the low-energy region less dependent on uncertain route estimates and improve the typical sampled topology, without adding quadratic terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
