<DESCRIPTION>
Switch relay-pressure normalization from mean incident demand to maximum incident demand, keeping the floor at 0.75 and leaving route-quality and reward settings unchanged. This isolates the normalization choice: the busiest node has pressure 1, while quieter nodes are discounted relative to it. Comparing this version against the current mean-based version tests whether the mean normalization is over-penalizing busy relays.
</DESCRIPTION>
