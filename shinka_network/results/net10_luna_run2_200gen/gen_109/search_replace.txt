<DESCRIPTION>
The current two-hop terms allocate an aggregate reward of 0.58 times each OD pair’s normalized demand, even when that reward is large relative to the direct-link reward. Cap that aggregate indirect reward at 35% of the corresponding direct-link value. This retains the existing support- and pressure-weighted route selection while limiting how strongly indirect paths can compete with direct service, which may improve the typical sampled topology without adding quadratic terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
