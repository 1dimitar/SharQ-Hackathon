<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current nonlinear component-link pressure discount with the milder linear factor `1 + 0.75 * edge_pressure`. The current exponent of 1.5 can strongly suppress paths using links with direct demand, even when those paths have good demand support. A moderate discount still favors less-loaded components while retaining more differentiation from the demand-supported path evidence, which may improve both the best and typical sampled topologies.
