increase_two_hop_strength
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the two-hop reward strength from 0.25 to 0.46 while keeping the demand transform and route-quality calculation unchanged. The current setting may underweight useful transit paths relative to direct-link rewards, especially for topologies where degree constraints prevent directly connecting every high-demand pair. A moderate increase should give the sampler more incentive to select links that form viable two-hop routes, without making the path reward as dominant as the previously tested 0.56 setting. This leaves the positive-demand-only path terms and their existing pressure discounts intact.
</DESCRIPTION>
