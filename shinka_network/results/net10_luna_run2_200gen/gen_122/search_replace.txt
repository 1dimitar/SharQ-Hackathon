narrow_path_quality
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current concave path support gives smaller OD demands substantially more relative influence than large demands. Narrow that variation by applying a bounded multiplier to each path’s existing support: clamp its ratio to normalized demand to [0.8, 1.4]. This preserves the current path-strength and transit-pressure terms while limiting how much concavity can distort the relative importance of OD pairs. For normalized demand in (0, 1], this mainly caps the boost given to small demands; the lower bound keeps the formulation well-defined if the demand model is later adjusted.
</DESCRIPTION>
