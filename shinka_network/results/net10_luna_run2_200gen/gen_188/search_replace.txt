<DESCRIPTION>
Lower the minimum relay-pressure factor from 0.75 to 0.65, leaving incident-demand calculation and edge discounting unchanged. This controlled test gives low-incident nodes more support as transit relays, which may help the sampled topologies route traffic without overloading a small set of high-pressure nodes. The change is limited to the pressure floor so its effect can be compared directly with the current setting.
</DESCRIPTION>
