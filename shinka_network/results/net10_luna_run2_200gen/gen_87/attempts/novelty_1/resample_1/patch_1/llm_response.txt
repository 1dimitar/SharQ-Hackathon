<DESCRIPTION>
Normalize transit-node pressure by the busiest node rather than by the mean, and use a 0.65 floor. This bounds pressure to a consistent [0.65, 1] range across demand matrices, while still discounting paths through the busiest transit nodes. It tests the suggested max-based normalization with a floor that preserves useful differentiation without suppressing most candidate paths.
</DESCRIPTION>
