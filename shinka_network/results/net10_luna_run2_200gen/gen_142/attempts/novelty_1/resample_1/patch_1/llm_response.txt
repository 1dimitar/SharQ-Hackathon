<DESCRIPTION>
Clamp the combined node- and edge-pressure discount to 0.7–1.5. The current discount can become very small for pressured transit nodes or large edge demands, suppressing useful two-hop alternatives and making the typical sampled topology less robust. A lower bound preserves some support for those routes, while the upper bound limits excessive rewards. This isolates the proposed multiplier-bound experiment without changing the linear terms or degree penalty.
</DESCRIPTION>
