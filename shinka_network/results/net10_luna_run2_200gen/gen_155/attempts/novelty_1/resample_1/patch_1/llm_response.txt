raise_relay_pressure_floor
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Raise the relay-pressure floor from 0.75 to 0.85. The current QUBO gives relatively strong rewards to two-hop paths through low-incident-demand nodes because their pressure is floored at 0.75. A higher floor discounts those paths more evenly, reducing the chance that the sampler over-rewards indirect routes and disrupts otherwise useful direct-link choices. This is a focused test of the suggested floor adjustment and leaves the path strength and edge discount unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
