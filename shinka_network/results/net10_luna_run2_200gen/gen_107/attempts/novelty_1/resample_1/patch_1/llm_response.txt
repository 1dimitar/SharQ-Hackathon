<DESCRIPTION>
The current two-hop reward applies across every possible intermediate node, so routes through relatively poor transit points can still receive substantial reward. This edit computes a quality for each candidate route from the existing transit-node and edge-pressure signals, normalizes those qualities to their per-OD mean, and clamps their relative influence to 0.8–1.4. The clamp limits how much any single route can dominate while still preferring better-supported, less-congested alternatives. The existing route reward, direct-link terms, and degree-penalty setting remain unchanged.
