<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the two-hop reward strength from 0.25 to 0.46 while leaving the direct-link rewards and pressure discounts unchanged. The current setting has a relatively strong best-sample gap but a much worse median gap, suggesting that useful multi-hop paths may be under-rewarded for typical samples. This is a measured increase that strengthens those path signals without jumping to the more aggressive 0.56 setting, which had mixed best-sample results.
