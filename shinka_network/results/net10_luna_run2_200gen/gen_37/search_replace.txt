<DESCRIPTION>
Use the maximum node load as the pressure normalizer and floor the inverse-pressure discount at 0.80. The current mean-based formula can strongly suppress paths through busy nodes, even though those nodes may be necessary to connect high-demand pairs. Maximum normalization makes the discount reflect relative pressure across the network, while the floor prevents it from overwhelming the two-hop demand reward. This is a targeted test of node-pressure scaling; all other terms remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
