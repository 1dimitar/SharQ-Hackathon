<DESCRIPTION>
After the existing route selection, estimate how much demand is assigned to each transit node by accumulating a small share of each selected route’s demand support. Then mildly reduce the reward for two-hop couplings through nodes whose estimated transit use is above average. This adds a local congestion signal without broadening the coupling graph or changing which routes are selected; the discount is capped so it cannot overwhelm the existing demand-based rewards. Reciprocal-link penalties remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
