<DESCRIPTION>
Add bounded endpoint-share evidence to route support. A leg is more informative when it accounts for a meaningful share of traffic leaving its source or entering its destination. The geometric combination of those shares is blended with the harmonic mean of the leg magnitudes; the harmonic mean remains an upper limit, so a strong leg cannot compensate for a weak one. Use the result mildly in candidate ranking and route rewards to prefer supported paths without eliminating alternatives.
</DESCRIPTION>
