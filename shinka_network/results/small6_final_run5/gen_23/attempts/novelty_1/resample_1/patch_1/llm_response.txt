<DESCRIPTION>
The current surrogate rewards a two-hop route even when its demand already has a direct link. This double counting can favor redundant local connectivity instead of useful reachability.

Model each route benefit as −reward × (1 − x_ik) × x_ij × x_jk. Approximate its cubic correction by a second-order expansion around link density p = 2/(n−1), retaining both quadratic and linear corrections. This introduces competition between a direct shortcut and its alternative route without auxiliary variables. The correction naturally weakens with network size, and the total coupling count remains below 4n³ even before duplicate keys are merged. This is a targeted experiment to improve topology and exchange ranking; its performance requires evaluation.
</DESCRIPTION>
