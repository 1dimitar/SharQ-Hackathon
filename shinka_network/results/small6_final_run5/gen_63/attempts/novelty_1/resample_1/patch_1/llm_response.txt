<DESCRIPTION>
Add a conservative second-pass congestion correction to the retained two-hop route rewards. Accumulate normalized demand on the selected intermediate nodes, then slightly discount route couplings that use the most-loaded intermediates. This complements the existing static and soft transit-pressure estimates with the usage implied by the sparse routes actually represented in the QUBO, without adding couplings or changing degree feasibility.
</DESCRIPTION>
