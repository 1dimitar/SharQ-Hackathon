<DESCRIPTION>
Two-hop route rewards become a less reliable proxy for actual load as the network grows: the number of possible routes increases, while only a small subset is rewarded. Attenuate both selected-route and fallback rewards gradually above n=10, with a floor to preserve their influence at larger sizes. Also make a small, independent reduction to degree-penalty growth. Feasibility is already high, so this modestly relaxes sampling pressure without removing the penalty that helps maintain valid topologies.
</DESCRIPTION>
