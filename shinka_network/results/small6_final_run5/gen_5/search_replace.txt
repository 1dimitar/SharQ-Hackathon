<DESCRIPTION>
Add a modest attractive coupling for reciprocal links when demand is substantial in both directions. The linear terms already reward each direction independently; this coupling captures the extra value of selecting both links together, giving the topology a useful two-way connection without rewarding a strong one-way demand as if it were bidirectional. The harmonic mean makes the bonus depend on both directions, and the coefficient keeps it moderate relative to the existing two-hop terms. This adds at most O(n²) couplings, well within the term limit.
</DESCRIPTION>
