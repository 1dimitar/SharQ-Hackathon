capped_three_hop_routes
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add at most one three-hop alternative for an OD pair when its two-hop options look weak or closely ranked, or the demand is in the top decile. Rank these alternatives using endpoint-leg affinity and transit-node pressure, then add a small second-order expansion of the selected path reward. This provides a limited signal for pairs that two-hop terms cannot represent, while keeping the added couplings sparse and capped well below the quadratic-term limit.
</DESCRIPTION>
