<DESCRIPTION>
Make the external degree penalty respond mildly to how unevenly incident demand is distributed. A high coefficient of variation signals that demand incentives are more uneven and can pull the sampler toward degree-imbalanced choices, so the penalty rises modestly; balanced or zero-demand instances use a lower value. The adjustment is bounded to [1.15, 1.35], retaining a narrow range around the current setting to avoid disrupting the strong feasible-sample rate.
</DESCRIPTION>
