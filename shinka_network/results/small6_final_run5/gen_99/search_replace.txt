size_tapered_route_incentive
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The two-hop reward terms can become a crowded signal as network size grows: many candidate routes compete for a topology with fixed degree two. Slightly tapering their strength with n may keep them informative without letting them dominate direct-link and structural signals, potentially improving both sample quality and energy-based ranking at larger sizes. Direct rewards, route selection, and cut correction remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
