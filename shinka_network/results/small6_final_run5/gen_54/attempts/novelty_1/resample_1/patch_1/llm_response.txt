<DESCRIPTION>
Add at most one extra two-hop route when the candidate just outside the usual selection is nearly tied with the boundary candidate. Such near-ties indicate uncertainty about which transit path will be useful, so modestly rewarding the alternative may improve sampling and give the post-processor a better energy ranking. The extra route gets a reduced weight, while the existing routes retain their previous rank boosts.
</DESCRIPTION>
