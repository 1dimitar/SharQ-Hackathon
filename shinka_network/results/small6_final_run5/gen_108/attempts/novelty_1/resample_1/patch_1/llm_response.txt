endpoint_share_blend
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Blend clipped endpoint-share evidence into the two route-leg supports before ranking candidate paths. A leg is more plausible when it represents a substantial share of its source’s outgoing demand or its destination’s incoming demand, even if its raw demand is moderate. The harmonic mean keeps the bilateral support sensitive to a weak leg, rather than letting one strong leg dominate. This isolates the effect on route selection while preserving the existing sparse coupling scheme.
</DESCRIPTION>
