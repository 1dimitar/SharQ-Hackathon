balanced_route_ranking
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Rank two-hop candidates using both their total leg demand and how evenly that support is split across the two legs. With the current sum-based ranking, a large value on just one leg can make a route look attractive even when the other leg has little demand support. The balance factor leaves well-supported, balanced routes essentially unchanged while lowering the rank of one-sided candidates. This changes candidate selection without adding couplings, and preserves the existing adaptive rule for adding a route when candidate ranks are close.
</DESCRIPTION>
