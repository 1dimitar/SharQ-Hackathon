<DESCRIPTION>
Keep the existing direct-link rewards and candidate-relay selection, but make two-hop support demand-dependent. OD pairs in the top 20% of positive demands receive a modest 1.1 multiplier, while other positive-demand pairs receive 0.9. This focuses relay couplings on the traffic most likely to dominate link load without increasing the quadratic-term count or changing the interaction structure.
