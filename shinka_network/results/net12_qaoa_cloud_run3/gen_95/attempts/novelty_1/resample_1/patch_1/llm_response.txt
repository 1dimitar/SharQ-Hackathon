<DESCRIPTION>
Concentrate a small amount of additional two-hop support on the most demanding OD pairs. Set the cutoff at the 90th percentile of positive off-diagonal demands, and give qualifying pairs one extra candidate relay, capped at the available number of relays. The existing relay ranking and reward formulas remain unchanged; the extra-relay cap is fixed at one. This tests whether focusing support on the strongest traffic improves topology quality without broadly increasing the number of QUBO couplings.
</DESCRIPTION>
