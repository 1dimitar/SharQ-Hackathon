<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the minimum number of candidate relays per positive-demand OD pair from two to four (when the network has enough intermediate nodes). The current sparse relay selection may omit useful two-hop routes, contributing to the poor median topology score. A modest increase adds alternatives for the QUBO to combine, while retaining the pressure-aware ranking and keeping the interaction count well below the allowed limit. For small networks, the budget remains capped by the number of available relays.
</DESCRIPTION>
