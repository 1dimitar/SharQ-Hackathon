The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

1. **Sweep the ordinary relay budget while keeping the current ranking and rewards fixed.** Compare `min(n-2, max(1, n//5))` and `min(n-2, n//3)` against the current `min(n-2, max(2, n//4))`. Generation 28’s 304-term compact support outperformed broader models, so test whether a smaller or moderately larger base budget improves the gaps without sacrificing its targeted structure.

This tests whether concentrating interactions further can improve the weaker best gap on `network12_s1` without broadly expanding the QUBO.

3. **Test a bottleneck-sensitive relay ranking.** Replace the geometric-mean affinity with `min(first_leg, second_leg) + 0.25 * max(first_leg, second_leg)`, retaining pressure discounting and the current relay budgets.
