<DESCRIPTION>
Rank candidate two-hop relays by the strength of their weaker leg, with a smaller contribution from the stronger leg. This favors paths whose two links both have useful demand support rather than paths with one dominant leg. Preserve the existing node- and edge-pressure discounts, and limit each OD pair to a deterministic relay budget; this keeps the interaction graph sparse while focusing quadratic rewards on the most promising paths.
</DESCRIPTION>
