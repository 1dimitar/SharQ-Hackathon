<DESCRIPTION>
The QUBO already rewards direct links and two-hop paths, but the evaluator reports that only 17% of sampled topologies are valid before repair. With the evaluator rescaling the cost terms to a maximum absolute weight of 1, a degree penalty of 1 can be too weak relative to the many cost terms: the solver may prefer an invalid topology that earns extra routing reward, leaving repair to fix it afterward. Raise the separate evaluator-supplied penalty to 6 so degree violations are substantially more expensive while preserving the existing cost-term ranking among valid topologies. This targets feasibility without adding quadratic terms or changing the cost model.
</DESCRIPTION>
