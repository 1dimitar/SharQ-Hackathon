<DESCRIPTION>
Raise the cutoff for the additional relay from the 80th to the 90th percentile of positive OD demands. This keeps the base relay count, two-relay cap, candidate ranking, and reward formulas unchanged, while reserving the extra interactions for the most important traffic pairs. The smaller set of extra relay terms may help prioritize high-impact routes without substantially increasing the QUBO’s interaction count.
</DESCRIPTION>
