<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the number of relay interactions for ordinary OD pairs, while retaining extra relay options for the heaviest demands. The current square-root budget gives every positive-demand pair the same number of relay couplings; fewer routine couplings may simplify the interaction communities and reduce competition among paths. A deterministic 80th-percentile cutoff allocates up to two extra relays to high-demand pairs, preserving additional routing support where it is most valuable.
</DESCRIPTION>
