<DESCRIPTION>
Use a softened power-law pressure discount for relay nodes. The current floor compresses the difference between lightly and heavily loaded relays; the offset power form keeps pressure-aware ranking while making its strength tunable. Choosing an exponent of 0.75 tests a moderate adjustment rather than an extreme one, and the resulting `node_pressure` is used directly by the existing quadratic weights.
</DESCRIPTION>
