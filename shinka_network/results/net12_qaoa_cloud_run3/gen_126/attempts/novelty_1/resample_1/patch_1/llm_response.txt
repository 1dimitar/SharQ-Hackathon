<DESCRIPTION>
Scale the two-hop reward by OD demand tier while leaving direct-link rewards, relay candidates, and relay budgets unchanged. OD pairs at or above the existing 80th-percentile cutoff receive a 1.1 multiplier; other positive-demand pairs receive 0.9. This modestly emphasizes relay paths for the largest flows without increasing the number of quadratic terms, testing whether stronger support for high-impact traffic improves the low-energy topologies.
</DESCRIPTION>
