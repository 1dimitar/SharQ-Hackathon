</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Raise normalized node pressure to 1.25 when computing relay pressure. Since pressure lies in [0.65, 1], this reduces the pressure discount for lower-pressure relays relative to the current linear factor, while leaving the highest-pressure nodes unchanged. That broadens the set of usable two-hop relays and may help the typical topology avoid over-relying on a narrow set of preferred paths. This is a focused sweep of pressure-discount strength; relay selection and term count remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
