<DESCRIPTION>
Add a modest, capped reward for two-link paths that can serve traffic not already well served by a direct link. Unlike an unrestricted two-hop reward, this discounts paths when the origin–destination demand is already covered directly, and caps each path’s contribution relative to the direct-link scale. The resulting couplings connect links that jointly provide useful routing options while limiting duplicated rewards that can encourage hub-heavy topologies. There is at most one term per directed two-hop path, well within the term limit. Keep the direct-demand linear terms and degree penalty unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
