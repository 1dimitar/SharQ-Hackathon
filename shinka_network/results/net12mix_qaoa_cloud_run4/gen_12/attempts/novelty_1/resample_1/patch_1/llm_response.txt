<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded two-hop reward, distributed across the possible relays for each OD pair rather than awarding the full demand to every candidate path. The relay weight uses the proposed endpoint factor, `0.25 + 0.75 * min(...)`, based on how strongly the adjacent links serve their endpoints. This gives endpoint suitability a useful but not exclusive influence: less-obvious relays still receive some credit, while promising two-hop routes can group their links through QUBO couplings. The total reward allocated for an OD pair is capped at 20% of its demand, so it supplements rather than overwhelms the direct-link signal. The added terms remain within the quadratic-term limit when combined with the existing terms.
