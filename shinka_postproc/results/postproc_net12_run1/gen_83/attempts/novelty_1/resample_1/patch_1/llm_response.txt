<DESCRIPTION>
When the search stagnates, select less-visited non-elite archive members more often instead of repeatedly exploring the incumbent’s basin. Add a small, bounded set of degree-preserving multi-switch kicks to each neighborhood; bias some switches toward priced links but leave others unrestricted. These proposals still pass through the existing energy and diversity screening before any LP calls, so dual information helps generate moves without being the sole selection criterion.
