<DESCRIPTION>
When the search stalls, diversify from archive members that have been selected least often rather than repeatedly branching from the current elite. Build a short degree-preserving kick by applying a few randomly chosen two-switches, then let the existing surrogate and dual-price screening decide which resulting neighbors merit LP calls. A bounded kick and retry limit keep this exploration from consuming excessive runtime, while ordinary iterations retain the existing selection behavior.
</DESCRIPTION>
