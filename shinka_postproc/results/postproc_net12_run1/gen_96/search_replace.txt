<DESCRIPTION>
Track whether searches from archive parents produce incumbent improvements, and use that yield to modestly favor productive parents. Keep a fixed 30% exploration share, choosing from the least-visited available parents so that distant or under-tested archive members continue to receive attention. This makes the selection rule more responsive than weighting by the tiny fractional size of an improvement, while retaining coverage.
</DESCRIPTION>
