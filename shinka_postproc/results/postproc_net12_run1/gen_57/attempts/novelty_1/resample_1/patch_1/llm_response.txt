<DESCRIPTION>
Track whether bottleneck-focused switches, unrestricted switches, and crossover proposals produce new incumbent bests. Adapt their proposal effort based on those measured outcomes, while keeping a minimum exploration share for every move type. This directs LP budget toward move types that are working without permanently abandoning alternatives that may help escape the current basin.
