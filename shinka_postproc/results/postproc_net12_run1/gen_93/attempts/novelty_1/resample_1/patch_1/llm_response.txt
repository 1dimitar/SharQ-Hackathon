local_parent_yield
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Measure each parent’s productivity by the best LP score found among its own batch relative to that parent, rather than only by improvement to the global incumbent. A parent can lead to useful local progress without setting a new global record; the current global-only statistic treats those discoveries as failures and may stop selecting productive regions. This change gives the existing parent-selection weights a more informative local-yield signal, while leaving the global-stagnation and rotation logic unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
