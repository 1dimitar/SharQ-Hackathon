<DESCRIPTION>
Track how often each archive parent is explored and whether its evaluated batches improve on that parent. Use these results to modestly favor productive parents, while reserving a share of archive selections for uniform exploration so less-tested and diverse members still get opportunities. This directs search effort using observed LP outcomes rather than relying only on archive score rank.
</DESCRIPTION>

            batches, gain_ema = parent_yield.get(net, (0, 0.0))
