<DESCRIPTION>
After sustained stagnation, deliberately sample a non-elite archive parent and build a short random walk of valid degree-preserving switches from it. This explores archive regions that ordinary parent selection and single-switch neighborhoods may undersample, while keeping the kick bounded to a few steps and drawing each step from the existing LP/price-guided switch generator. The kicked topology is included in the existing batch screening, so it consumes an LP call only if it survives the normal freshness and validity checks.
</DESCRIPTION>
