<DESCRIPTION>
Vectorize the batched all-pairs shortest-path proxy calculation. It computes the same demand-weighted distances but avoids running a separate Python-level Floyd–Warshall loop for every shortlisted network, reducing screening overhead and helping the search stay within the wall-time limit.

Also scale multi-switch kicks with the selected parent’s stall count and network size. Recently improving parents retain the existing modest kick depth; repeatedly unproductive parents receive more kick attempts and longer perturbations, giving the search a better chance to leave local basins without imposing that cost throughout the run.
</DESCRIPTION>
