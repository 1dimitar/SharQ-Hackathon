<DESCRIPTION>
The current generator already proposes switches involving bottleneck links, but its LP-batch shortlist can favor energy-ranked or random moves and fail to spend calls on those proposals. Use the magnitude of positive LP prices to score how strongly a move removes priced arcs, with a normalized high-load fallback when prices are unavailable. Preserve that score when proposals are duplicated or combined into walks and rotations. Then reserve shortlist and LP-batch capacity for moves with the greatest bottleneck-arc removal. This makes dual guidance affect which networks are actually certified, while retaining the existing energy, routing, and exploratory selections.
</DESCRIPTION>
