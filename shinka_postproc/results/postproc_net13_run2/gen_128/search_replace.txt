<DESCRIPTION>
Protect one archive-relinking intermediate from being crowded out by surrogate-ranked moves. The current code generates short paths toward archive members, but `select_batch` may never evaluate those intermediates if their energy ranking is poor. This change records the current parent's relinking steps and reserves one batch slot for a randomly chosen step, while still allowing the remaining slots to favor low-energy and diverse proposals. Targets are sampled from the full set of sufficiently distant archive members rather than only a small quality-ranked subset, giving bridges between different regions a chance to be tested.
</DESCRIPTION>
