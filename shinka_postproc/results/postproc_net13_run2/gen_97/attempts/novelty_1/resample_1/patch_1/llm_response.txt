<DESCRIPTION>
Track which move operators produced each candidate and update a smoothed success weight from LP-confirmed improvements over the current parent. Use those weights to reserve part of each small batch for operators with better recent results, while retaining the existing energy-ranked selection to fill the rest. This adapts the search effort to the pool’s observed behavior without permanently eliminating less-tested operators.
