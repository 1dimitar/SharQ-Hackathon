<DESCRIPTION>
Strengthen stalled-search escapes in two complementary ways. Instead of proposing only isolated switches toward elite networks, perform short, degree-preserving path-relinking walks toward several distant elites and retain intermediate networks. Also increase both the number and length of random multi-switch kicks as a parent accumulates stalls. This gives the search a better chance to cross barriers while keeping the extra proposals screened before they consume LP calls.
</DESCRIPTION>
