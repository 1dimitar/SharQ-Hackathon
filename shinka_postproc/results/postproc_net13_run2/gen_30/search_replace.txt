adaptive_parent_productivity
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Track LP evaluations and score improvement magnitude for each parent, rather than relying only on whether a batch improved. Select parents using a smoothed improvement rate, with an exposure penalty so that repeatedly searched parents lose priority while underexplored archive members still receive trials. This makes the available LP budget respond to measured search productivity and preserves exploration across distinct regions.
</DESCRIPTION>
