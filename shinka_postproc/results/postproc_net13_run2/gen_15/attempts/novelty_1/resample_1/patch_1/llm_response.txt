</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
When a parent’s neighborhood repeatedly fails to improve, the current screening still spends all three LP evaluations on the best surrogate-ranked moves. That can over-trust a loosely correlated surrogate and repeatedly explore similar choices. Keep the two highest-ranked moves, but reserve the third slot for a randomly selected, topologically distinct fresh move once the parent has stalled at least twice. This uses the existing per-parent stall signal to adapt the exploration mix while preserving the normal screening behavior for productive parents.
</DESCRIPTION>
