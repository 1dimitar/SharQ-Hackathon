<DESCRIPTION>
Rank screened moves first by their effect on the parent’s positive-price links, then by surrogate-energy change. A two-switch that removes a bottleneck link without adding another is more promising for reducing the LP objective than an energy-favored move that leaves the bottlenecks untouched. Keeping energy as the secondary criterion retains the existing pool-specific guidance, while the unchanged batch size limits the extra LP-call cost. Pass the already-computed LP detail into screening at both call sites.

    def screen_batch(moves, parent, detail, batch_size=9):
        """Prioritize moves that improve bottleneck links, then surrogate energy."""
        fresh = [
            m for m in moves
            if m not in seen and not tools.is_cached(m)
        ]
        if not fresh:
            return []

        prices = detail.get("prices", {}) if detail else {}
        hot = {tuple(map(int, a)) for a, p in prices.items() if p > 0}
        base_e = energy(parent)
        ranked = []
        for net in fresh:
            # A negative delta means the move removes more priced links than it adds.
            hot_delta = len(set(net) & hot) - len(set(parent) & hot)
            ranked.append((hot_delta, energy(net) - base_e, net))
        ranked.sort(key=lambda item: (item[0], item[1]))
