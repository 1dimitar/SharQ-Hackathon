<DESCRIPTION>
Track whether two-switches, three-link rotations, or multi-switch kicks produce improvements, then allocate more generation effort to move classes with better observed improvement rates. A small exploration floor keeps every neighborhood active, while a modest cap prevents early noisy results from starving other move types. The classification uses the number of changed arcs, so it requires no changes to move-generation interfaces.
</DESCRIPTION>
