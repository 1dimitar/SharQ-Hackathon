<DESCRIPTION>
Adapt the number of proposals generated by the four move classes—focused switches, unrestricted switches, rotations, and multi-switch kicks—using whether recent batches improved their parent. A successful batch modestly rewards the currently sampled mix; an unsuccessful batch shifts capacity toward rotations and kicks to encourage basin escape. Minimum allocations preserve a balanced mix, and the total proposal-generation budget stays bounded. This adds feedback to the existing stall-based rotation escalation without changing move validity or LP scoring.
