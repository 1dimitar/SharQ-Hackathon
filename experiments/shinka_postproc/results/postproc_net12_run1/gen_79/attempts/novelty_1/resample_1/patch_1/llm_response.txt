<DESCRIPTION>
Periodically branch from an underexplored non-elite archive member rather than repeatedly sampling the same strong states. Apply a short sequence of degree-preserving two-switches, preferring switches that touch bottleneck links but falling back to unrestricted switches when needed. This adds bounded exploration of distinct basins while preserving validity and the existing LP-call screening process.
