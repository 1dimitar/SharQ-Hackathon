<DESCRIPTION>
The current LP batch is chosen almost entirely by surrogate energy, so its calls can cluster around near-duplicate topologies and miss useful alternatives. Select energy-ranked proposals while first enforcing a minimum edge-set distance; then fill remaining slots by energy rank. Also fix the progress check: `evaluate` updates `best_score`, so comparing the returned value against `best_score` immediately afterward can never detect a new best. Comparing against the score before evaluation lets the search reset its stale counter when it actually improves.
</DESCRIPTION>
