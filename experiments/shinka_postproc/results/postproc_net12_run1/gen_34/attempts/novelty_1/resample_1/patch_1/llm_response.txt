<DESCRIPTION>
Rank proposed moves using the directional LP-price signal: removing positively priced links and adding unpriced links is a promising way to reduce congestion. Reserve part of each LP batch for the strongest price-benefit moves, while retaining energy-ranked and diverse selections for exploration. This makes dual guidance affect which networks receive LP calls without letting the surrogate or prices become a hard filter.
