<DESCRIPTION>
Add path-relinking moves that take a single switch toward another high-quality archived topology. For each selected target, the search looks for target links absent from the current parent and tries to install them with a degree-preserving two-switch. This explores connections between promising regions that random neighborhoods may miss, while preserving validity and leaving the existing LP batch screening in place.
