<DESCRIPTION>
Adapt the random switch, rotation, and multi-switch-kick proposal effort using their observed returns. Track how often each move class produces a network better than its parent, then allocate a fixed proposal budget according to smoothed success rates. The exhaustive bottleneck-focused switch neighborhood remains unchanged, so adaptation preserves that reliable source of moves while shifting exploratory effort toward move types that are productive in the current search.
