<DESCRIPTION>
Increase three-edge rotation diversity only after repeated unsuccessful batches, using `kick_size` as the existing stagnation signal. In this stronger phase, most sampled rotations include a priced bottleneck edge, while the other two edges remain exploratory. When an improvement resets `kick_size`, rotation generation returns to its normal level. This targets the recommended escape move without spending extra LP calls or changing the search during productive periods.
</DESCRIPTION>
