directional_swap_order
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Rank exchanges by the priced load removed from the network, then discount moves that add links with high direct demand. This gives bottleneck-focused descent a directional estimate of both sides of a swap, while retaining the existing randomized tie order. The estimate is only used to order LP evaluations; the LP still decides whether a move improves the network.
</DESCRIPTION>
