<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded kick when the global best has not improved for 12 search iterations. The kick starts from a strong but non-elite archive member, preserving the best solutions while exploring a different basin. Its size increases gradually across repeated stalls and resets after an improvement, so exploration escalates without turning every iteration into a large random perturbation.
</DESCRIPTION>
