parent_stagnation_rotations
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Escalate three-edge rotations selectively for parents whose neighborhoods repeatedly fail to improve that parent. The existing global kick size does not distinguish a repeatedly exhausted basin from a newly selected parent; a per-parent failure count does. After several unsuccessful batches, this increases the move-generation effort (including rotations involving priced links), while successful parents reset their count. The escalation is capped to keep proposal-generation time bounded.
</DESCRIPTION>
