<DESCRIPTION>
Track how often each archive network is selected and how often its search batch improves on that parent. Select parents with an upper-confidence-style bonus: strong basins remain attractive, while under-tested archive members receive exploration weight. This allocates LP calls adaptively instead of repeatedly sampling parents using only their archive rank, and retains explicit exploration of distant archive entries.
