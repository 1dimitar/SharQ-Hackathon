productive_branch_allocation
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Keep a small archive of distinct promising networks and track how often each branch produces an improvement. Cap each descent’s 2-exchange scan so one branch cannot consume the entire LP budget; then allocate subsequent searches more often to branches with better improvement histories, while still occasionally exploring less-proven branches. This applies the branch-productivity recommendation and replaces the current best-only kick loop, which repeatedly searches around the incumbent and can neglect other useful starting networks.
</DESCRIPTION>
