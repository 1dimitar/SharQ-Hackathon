<DESCRIPTION>
Use the LP dual prices directionally when screening moves: a switch is promising when it removes highly priced links and adds low-priced ones. Keep the existing energy-ranked batch, but reserve a few additional LP evaluations for moves with the strongest positive price benefit. This preserves the surrogate’s useful signal while ensuring promising dual-guided moves are not crowded out by energy ranking.
</DESCRIPTION>
