<DESCRIPTION>
The current iterated search always kicks from the incumbent, which can repeatedly revisit the same basin. Keep a small archive of strong, structurally distinct networks and, after sustained stagnation, launch bounded kicks from a non-elite archive member. Increase kick size gradually with further stagnation, then locally descend as before. This adds basin changes without replacing the incumbent-focused search or spending extra LP calls on the kick itself; only the resulting valid network and descent proposals are scored.
</DESCRIPTION>
