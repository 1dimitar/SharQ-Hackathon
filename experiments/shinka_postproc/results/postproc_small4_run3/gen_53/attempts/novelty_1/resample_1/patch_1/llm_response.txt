<DESCRIPTION>
The archive is currently ordered almost entirely by LP score, so its first few entries can dominate parent selection even when low-energy or routing-proxy-leading networks represent useful, different search regions. Reserve archive places for those two feature groups as well as the existing LP elites and structurally separated networks. Then select parents explicitly across LP, energy, routing-proxy, and diverse groups, with visit-based weighting within each group. This preserves multiple sources of promising structure without changing move generation or spending additional LP calls.
