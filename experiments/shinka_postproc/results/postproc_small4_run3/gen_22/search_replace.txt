<DESCRIPTION>
When a parent stalls, increase the number and depth of chained switch walks, and bias relinking toward a structurally distant archive member. This gives stalled searches a stronger way to leave their current neighborhood while retaining the existing, lighter behavior for productive parents. The extra effort is capped and scales with \(n\).
</DESCRIPTION>
