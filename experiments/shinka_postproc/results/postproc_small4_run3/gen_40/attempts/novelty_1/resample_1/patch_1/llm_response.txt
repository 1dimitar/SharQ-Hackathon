<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the removal-pressure ranking with a cheap routing-based screen. The current pressure sum favors larger perturbations simply because they remove more links, without estimating whether their replacements actually help traffic.

First rank moves by the demand-weighted shortest-path lower bound. Then estimate equal-cost shortest-path link loads for a small shortlist combining good energy, good hop count, and random moves. Reserve two LP slots for low estimated congestion, while retaining energy-led exploitation and random exploration. This introduces an independent, demand-aware signal without fitting an online model or letting bottleneck guidance dominate. All shortlist sizes scale with n; the routing estimate is only a heuristic, and certification remains exclusively through tools.
</DESCRIPTION>
