<DESCRIPTION>
Make bottleneck removal a focused move source rather than relying on random rotations to happen to include a hot link. Track switches and rotations that remove positive-price or highest-load arcs, add sampled three-arc rotations that deliberately include a hot arc, and reserve one LP evaluation per batch for the best-energy fresh focused move. The rest of the batch selection remains unchanged, so hot-arc guidance supplements rather than dominates exploration. All added move counts scale with `n`.
