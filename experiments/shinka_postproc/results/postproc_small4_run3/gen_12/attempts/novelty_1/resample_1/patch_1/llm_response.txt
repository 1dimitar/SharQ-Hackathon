<DESCRIPTION>
Balance archive exploration using each parent’s visit count, while retaining the existing elite-selection branch. This reduces repeated sampling of the same regions without discarding good networks. Also increase the number and strength of multi-switch kicks when a parent stalls, giving stalled searches a larger chance to escape local optima. Both changes adapt to the instance size and use no additional LP calls.
</DESCRIPTION>
