<DESCRIPTION>
Add a small uncertainty-aware exploration component to the existing online ridge model. Its prediction uncertainty is estimated from the regularized feature-space leverage, so candidates in poorly sampled regions can receive attention without replacing the energy, routing, and novelty signals. Reserve one LP slot for a candidate whose predicted score remains competitive but whose uncertainty improves its lower-confidence-bound ranking. The other batch slots and the existing selection signals remain in place.
</DESCRIPTION>
