<DESCRIPTION>
Add a complementary routing estimate that sends traffic along shortest paths using three deterministic tie-breakings: lowest next-node ID, highest next-node ID, and a node/destination parity rule. Use the 75th-percentile and worst-case peak link loads across these routings as extra candidate features. These are ranking signals only: the search still retains its existing energy, learned, novelty, and random selections, while reserving one LP evaluation slot for candidates ranked by the tie-robust estimate. This can identify networks whose apparent quality depends on an unusually favorable equal-split routing, without excluding them from other selection channels.
</DESCRIPTION>
