<DESCRIPTION>
Continue a search from a batch’s best improving neighbor instead of always choosing the next parent afresh from the archive. This adds a short local-improvement trajectory through promising networks, while retaining archive-based exploration on the remaining rounds. It also records the best improvement relative to the actual parent, avoiding a separate batch-wide minimum calculation.
</DESCRIPTION>
