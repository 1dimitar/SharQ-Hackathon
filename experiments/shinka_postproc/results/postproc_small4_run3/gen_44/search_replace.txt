adaptive_source_quotas

<DESCRIPTION>
Adapt the LP batch allocation among energy, learned prediction, routing proxy, novelty, and exploration using each source’s recent rate of beating the selected parent. Every source starts with a protected slot when enough candidates are available; remaining slots are allocated in proportion to smoothed success rates. This preserves the existing multi-signal portfolio while allowing the search to favor signals that are working on the current pool, without committing to one heuristic or tuning quotas to a particular network size.
</DESCRIPTION>
