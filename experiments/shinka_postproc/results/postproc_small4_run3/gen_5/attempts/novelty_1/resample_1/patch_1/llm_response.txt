<DESCRIPTION>
Use the parent network’s LP dual prices to rank proposed moves by the bottleneck pressure they may remove. For each proposal, estimate relief from removed priced links and subtract a small, scale-normalized penalty for direct demand on newly added links. This complements the energy model and the existing count of changed hot links, while keeping the estimate cheap and requiring no additional LP calls. It is a heuristic rather than a prediction of the new routing, so it only changes which proposals get LP budget—not which networks are accepted.
</DESCRIPTION>
