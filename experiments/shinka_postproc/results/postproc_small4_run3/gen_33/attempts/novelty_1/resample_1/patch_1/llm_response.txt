<DESCRIPTION>
The current two-switch neighborhood already considers every swap involving a hot arc, but its three-arc rotations choose arcs uniformly at random, so they may rarely include a bottleneck. Add a size-scaled set of rotations that explicitly anchors one of the parent’s hot arcs and samples two partner arcs. These degree-preserving moves remove the flagged arc while offering alternative links, and are then screened alongside the existing random rotations, kicks, and other proposals. This keeps the added work modest as n grows and preserves the broader move portfolio.
