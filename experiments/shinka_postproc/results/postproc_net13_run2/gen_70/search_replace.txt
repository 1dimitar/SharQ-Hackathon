<DESCRIPTION>
The current batch selector is dominated by surrogate-ranked proposals, so it can repeatedly spend LP calls in familiar parts of the topology space. Reserve one batch slot for the shortlisted proposal whose nearest-archive symmetric difference is greatest. This preserves the existing surrogate and diversity selection while ensuring that each batch tests a structurally novel option; if the most novel proposal is already selected, the batch is unchanged.
