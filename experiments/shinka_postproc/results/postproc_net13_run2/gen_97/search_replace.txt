<DESCRIPTION>
The neighborhood generator already focuses many proposals on links with positive LP dual prices, but the current batch screening then ranks moves only by surrogate energy. This edit carries the bottleneck signal into batch selection: reserve up to three LP evaluations for diverse, surrogate-promising proposals that alter active bottleneck links, then fill the remaining batch using the existing energy ranking. It keeps the surrogate useful for screening while ensuring that useful bottleneck-focused moves are not crowded out by proposals that look better only under the approximate Hamiltonian.
