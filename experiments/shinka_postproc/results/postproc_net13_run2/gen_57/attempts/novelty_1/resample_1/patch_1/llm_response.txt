<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the mostly fixed elite-versus-stall parent choice with progress- and exploration-aware selection. Track how often each archived network is selected and how often its neighborhood produces an improvement. Weight parents by a blend of score rank, under-selection, structural isolation, and demonstrated search progress. This retains pressure toward strong networks while giving under-explored regions a chance to produce useful moves.

    def select_parent(pop):
        # Balance quality, structural isolation, past productivity, and exploration.
        if len(pop) == 1:
            parent = pop[0]
        else:
            weights = []
            for rank, net in enumerate(pop):
                novelty = min(
                    (distance(net, other) for other in pop if other != net),
                    default=0,
                ) / max(1, 2 * n)
                underexplored = 1.0 / ((1.0 + parent_visits.get(net, 0)) ** 0.45)
                productive = 1.0 + min(1.0, 0.35 * parent_gains.get(net, 0))
                quality = 1.0 / (1.0 + 0.10 * rank)
                isolation = 0.75 + 0.5 * novelty

                    quality * isolation * productive * underexplored
