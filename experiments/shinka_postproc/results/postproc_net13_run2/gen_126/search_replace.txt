<DESCRIPTION>
Every sixth search round, reserve one LP evaluation for a legal two-switch step toward a randomly selected, distant archive member. Unlike ordinary moves, this relinking candidate is evaluated before surrogate-ranked local proposals, so it cannot be crowded out by them. Shuffling the distant targets gives varied archive members an opportunity to contribute rather than repeatedly favoring only the highest-ranked few. The relinking step is accepted as a proposal only when it increases overlap with its target; all moves preserve the network’s degree constraints.
</DESCRIPTION>

        """Return a legal two-switch that moves source closer to target."""
        cur = set(source)
        target_edges = set(target)
        arcs = list(source)
        current_overlap = len(cur & target_edges)
        best_gain = 0
        best_moves = []
