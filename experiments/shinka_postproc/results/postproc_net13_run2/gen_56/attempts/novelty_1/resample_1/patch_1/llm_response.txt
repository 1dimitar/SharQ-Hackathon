<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded archive-guided proposal operator. It examines degree-preserving two-switches and favors switches whose new links occur in strong, structurally diverse archived networks—especially when both links co-occur in the same archive member. Reserve at most one slot in each LP batch for the best fresh guided proposal, so these proposals receive a fair test without displacing the existing search or overwhelming it with recombination.
</DESCRIPTION>
