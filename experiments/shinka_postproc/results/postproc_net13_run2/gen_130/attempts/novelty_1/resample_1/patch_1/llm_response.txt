<DESCRIPTION>
Add degree-preserving path-relinking proposals between the current parent and a network in the evaluated archive. A relinking step inserts an arc from the target by switching the parent’s outgoing arc from its tail with an arc entering its head; this preserves all in/out degrees and moves the topology toward a known-good network. The existing multi-switch kick remains as a fallback, so relinking supplements rather than replaces broader exploration.
