<DESCRIPTION>
Reserve one LP evaluation in each screened batch for a structurally novel move. Surrogate-ranked moves remain the main selection mechanism, but a move far from the evaluated archive may reveal a good region that the surrogate ranking would otherwise overlook. Choose the novel move by maximizing its minimum symmetric-difference distance from the archive; because the ranked list is surrogate-ordered, ties naturally favor the better surrogate score. The batch remains bounded by its existing size.
</DESCRIPTION>
