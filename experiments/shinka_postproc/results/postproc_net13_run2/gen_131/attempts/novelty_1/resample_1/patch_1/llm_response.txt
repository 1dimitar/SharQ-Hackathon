<DESCRIPTION>
Relinking paths are currently mixed into the general move set, so surrogate screening can discard every bridge step in a batch. Track the relinking intermediates separately and reserve one LP evaluation for a fresh bridge whenever one is available. Also choose the target from the full set of sufficiently distant archive members rather than repeatedly favoring the first few score-ranked targets. This preserves a chance to test relinking progress while varying which strong, structurally distinct region it connects to.
</DESCRIPTION>
