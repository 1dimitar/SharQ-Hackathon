<DESCRIPTION>
The current path relinking follows just one randomly chosen archive target, so useful intermediate networks can be missed when that path stalls or its early steps are poor. Replace it with short paths toward several strong, distinct targets. At each step, retain the best-overlap switches as candidate moves and randomly choose among them to continue the path. This gives the existing surrogate-based batch screening more target-directed alternatives without spending LP calls on every path step.
</DESCRIPTION>
