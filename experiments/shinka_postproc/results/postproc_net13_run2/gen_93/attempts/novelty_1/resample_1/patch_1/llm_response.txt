operator_adaptive_trials
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Track stall signals separately for bottleneck switches, random switches, rotations, and archive recombination. Attribute each proposed move to its generating operator, then update an operator’s stall count only when a move from that operator was actually LP-scored. Increase proposal effort for operators whose scored moves have stopped beating their parent, with caps to keep generation bounded. This gives underexplored or stalled move types more chances without letting a parent’s overall stall signal incorrectly drive every neighborhood operator.
</DESCRIPTION>
