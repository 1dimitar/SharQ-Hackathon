<DESCRIPTION>
Track generated proposals, LP evaluations, improvements, and improvement magnitude for three move families: two-switches, rotations, and kicks/path relinking. Use the observed LP outcomes to adapt how many sampled proposals each family generates, while retaining a minimum share for every family. This puts the feedback on the expensive, exact objective rather than surrogate energy alone, and avoids allowing a temporarily lucky operator to eliminate exploration of the others.
