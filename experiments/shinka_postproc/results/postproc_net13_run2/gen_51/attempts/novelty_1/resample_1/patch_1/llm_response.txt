<DESCRIPTION>
Add a bounded archive-guided matching operator. Every sixth search round, it builds a candidate from two randomized bipartite matchings: each matching gives every source one outgoing link and every destination one incoming link. A backtracking assignment excludes self-loops and duplicate links, so the combined network is valid by construction. Destination choices are biased toward links shared by strong LP-scored archive members. The candidate joins the existing move pool and is screened alongside other proposals, keeping this exploratory operator bounded rather than replacing the established search.
</DESCRIPTION>
