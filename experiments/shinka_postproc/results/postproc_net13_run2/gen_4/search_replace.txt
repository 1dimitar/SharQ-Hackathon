<DESCRIPTION>
Use the LP’s link prices to rank proposed moves by whether they remove expensive links and add less expensive ones, while retaining surrogate energy as a tie-breaker. Positive dual prices identify links currently contributing to the congestion bound, so this gives the LP batch a more directly relevant signal than energy alone. The existing diversity selection and batch limit remain unchanged, preserving exploration and the fixed LP budget.
