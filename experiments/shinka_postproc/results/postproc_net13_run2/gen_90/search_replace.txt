<DESCRIPTION>
The archive already mixes elite parents with the rest, but its visit adjustment can strongly suppress parents that have been explored repeatedly—even if they belong to a promising, distinct region. Keep the 48% elite preference and the existing productivity signal, while giving the remaining archive members an explicit inverse-square-root visit bonus. This favors underexplored regions without making selection purely novelty-driven or discarding evidence of past improvements.
