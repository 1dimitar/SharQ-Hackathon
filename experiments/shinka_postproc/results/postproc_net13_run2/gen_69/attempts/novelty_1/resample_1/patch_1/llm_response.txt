<DESCRIPTION>
Reserve one LP evaluation per batch for the valid, uncached move that is most structurally different from the scored archive. The current batch selector favors surrogate rank and diversity within a shortlist, but may still omit a novel move elsewhere in the neighborhood. Replacing the final selected move preserves the promising choices while explicitly testing a new region; surrogate rank breaks ties in novelty.
</DESCRIPTION>
