<DESCRIPTION>
Keep the existing set of demand-supported two-hop paths and their overall scale, but discount paths through high-traffic relay nodes. A relay’s row-plus-column demand is a simple proxy for how much transit load it may already need to carry; the stronger `1 / (1 + node_load[j] / max_node_load)` discount tests whether avoiding busy relays improves the typical as well as best sampled topology. This adds no quadratic terms and leaves direct-link rewards and degree penalties unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
