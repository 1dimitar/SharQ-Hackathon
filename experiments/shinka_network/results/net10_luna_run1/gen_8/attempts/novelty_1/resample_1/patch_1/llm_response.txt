<DESCRIPTION>
The current two-hop terms reward every selected two-link path, which can favor topologies that route traffic through the same busy intermediaries. Replace that reward with a modest positive congestion cost: selecting both links of a path incurs more cost when its demand is high and the intermediate node has heavy inbound or outbound traffic. This retains the direct-demand signal while discouraging especially risky transit paths, without exceeding the existing O(n³) term count.
</DESCRIPTION>
