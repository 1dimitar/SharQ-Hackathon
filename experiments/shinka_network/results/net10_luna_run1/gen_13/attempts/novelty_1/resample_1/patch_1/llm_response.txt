Replace the per-intermediate averaging and node-congestion discount with a modest, uniform reward for each demand-supported two-hop path. The current factors make each quadratic coefficient especially small, so these terms may have little influence relative to direct-link rewards. Using `-0.08 * demand[i, k] / scale` keeps the same sparse set of interactions while giving selected paths a stronger signal. The coefficient is deliberately well below the previously tried strong two-hop reward, which performed worse than the direct-demand baseline.
