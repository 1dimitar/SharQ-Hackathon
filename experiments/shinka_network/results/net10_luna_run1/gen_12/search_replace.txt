<DESCRIPTION>
Replace maximum-based normalization with a robust 90th-percentile scale. Negative demand and self-links are excluded, and values above the percentile are clipped. This prevents a single unusually large demand from dominating the linear rewards, while retaining the relative ranking of demand-supported links below the clipping threshold. The existing degree penalty remains unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
