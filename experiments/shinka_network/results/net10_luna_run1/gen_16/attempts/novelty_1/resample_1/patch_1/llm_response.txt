<DESCRIPTION>
Measure relay usefulness using the share of traffic entering node j that comes from i and the share leaving j that goes to k. The current inbound factor divides by i’s total outgoing traffic, which can favor links that are common destinations for i without establishing that j is a meaningful receiving relay. Normalizing by j’s incoming traffic instead makes the path-specific factor reflect both sides of the relay. Keep the existing small background reward and congestion discount so the change adjusts, rather than replaces, the current two-hop signal.
</DESCRIPTION>
