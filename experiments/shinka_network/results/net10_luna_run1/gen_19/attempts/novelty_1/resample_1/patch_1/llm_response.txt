<DESCRIPTION>
The current QUBO gives direct demand a coefficient of −1.0 and two-hop paths a coefficient of 0.12. Sweep toward a less dominant direct reward and a moderately stronger path reward: use −0.8 for direct links and 0.16 for qualifying two-hop paths. This preserves the existing positive-endpoint-demand and traffic-share filters while testing whether the low-energy topologies better account for traffic that can be carried through relays.
</DESCRIPTION>
