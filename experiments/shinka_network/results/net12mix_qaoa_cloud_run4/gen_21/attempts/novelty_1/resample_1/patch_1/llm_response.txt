<DESCRIPTION>
The indirect reward currently spreads each OD pair’s fixed reward across nearly every possible relay, creating a dense interaction graph of about 840 terms. That broad signal may be too weakly selective to justify its influence relative to direct-demand rewards. Reduce its scale from 0.15 to 0.10 while preserving the existing allocation and coupling structure; this tests whether a more conservative indirect signal improves the sampled topologies without adding terms or changing the degree penalty.
</DESCRIPTION>
