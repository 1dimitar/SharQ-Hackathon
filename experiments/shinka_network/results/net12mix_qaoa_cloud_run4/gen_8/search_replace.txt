<DESCRIPTION>
Add modest quadratic rewards for two-link paths, weighted by the relay node’s balanced in/out traffic score raised to the power 1.5. This makes indirect-routing rewards more selective than a uniform two-hop bonus, while retaining the direct-demand linear baseline. Excluding paths that return to their starting node avoids rewarding unproductive two-cycles. The added terms are O(n³), within the term limit.
</DESCRIPTION>
