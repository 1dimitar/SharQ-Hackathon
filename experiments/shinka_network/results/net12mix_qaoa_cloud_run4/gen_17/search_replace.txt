<DESCRIPTION>
Add modest positive couplings between links sharing a sender or receiver, weighted by that node’s share of total traffic. With only two outgoing and two incoming links per node, these couplings discourage concentrating transit capacity at traffic-heavy endpoints. The per-pair normalization keeps this congestion signal selective rather than letting the added terms overwhelm the existing two-hop rewards. It also creates interactions among links that can help the partitioned solver explore endpoint-spread topologies.
</DESCRIPTION>
