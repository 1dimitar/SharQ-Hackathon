<DESCRIPTION>
Use two affinity-selected routes per demand, admitting a third only when it is nearly tied with the second. Apply a stricter admission ratio of 0.96 to ordinary demands while retaining 0.92 for the highest-demand quartile.

This limits redundant path rewards that can favor poor topologies and distort exchange rankings. Heavy demands retain more routing flexibility, and the existing low-pressure fallback remains unchanged. Compare traffic against the traffic-derived cutoff so the high-demand classification is scale invariant. Structural terms, reward strengths, and degree penalties remain unchanged to isolate the route-selection change.
</DESCRIPTION>
