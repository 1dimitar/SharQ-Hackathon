<DESCRIPTION>
Estimate each intermediary’s potential transit burden from demand-weighted two-hop routes, using the harmonic mean of the two leg demands so a route with a weak leg contributes little. Convert the normalized burden into a bounded discount, then use it both when ranking intermediary candidates and when setting their route incentives. This discourages concentrating routes through already-busy transit nodes without letting congestion overwhelm the existing direct-demand and path terms. The extra work is O(n³), matching the existing route-candidate loop.
</DESCRIPTION>
