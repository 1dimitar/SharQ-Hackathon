weak_leg_discount
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Discount each selected two-hop route when its weaker leg has little demand support. The bounded factor is based on the smaller normalized demand of the two legs, so a route with one weak leg receives less reward even if the other leg is strong; well-supported routes retain the existing reward. This leaves candidate selection, sparse coupling count, path-affinity weighting, and transit-pressure discount unchanged, while reducing the influence of poorly supported shortcuts on both sampling and energy-based ranking.
</DESCRIPTION>
