<DESCRIPTION>
Discount route rewards at intermediate nodes that already carry many strong retained routes. The accumulated absolute route-coupling magnitude is demand-weighted by the existing route support and selection factors, so it provides a lightweight proxy for competition without adding new couplings or changing the route-selection logic. The discount is bounded to at most 18%, preserving the existing signal while making heavily used transit nodes somewhat less attractive.
</DESCRIPTION>

    if peak_route_usage > 1e-15:
        for key, middle in route_nodes.items():
