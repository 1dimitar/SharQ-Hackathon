<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the number of two-hop alternatives while leaving their ranking and weight calculation unchanged. The current QUBO contains roughly 400 quadratic terms, and lower-ranked alternatives can add noisy attractions without providing much additional route information. Keep one route for ordinary-demand pairs and two for high-demand pairs; the existing near-tie rule can still retain one extra when the choice is ambiguous. This tests a sparser service-pair budget while preserving the existing service scoring.
</DESCRIPTION>
