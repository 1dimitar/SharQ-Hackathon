ambiguous_third_route
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Limit each demand to its two best two-hop routes by default. For demands above the existing 68th-percentile cutoff, add a third route only when its rank is within 8% of the second route’s rank. This targets genuinely ambiguous routing choices while dropping the unconditional low-pressure fallback and many weaker couplings, keeping the model more compact and reducing rewards for poorly supported routes.
</DESCRIPTION>
