<DESCRIPTION>
Estimate transit congestion from each OD pair’s two best plausible two-hop routes, weighted by their leg evidence and current node pressure. Blend this projected load modestly with each node’s existing incident traffic before ranking route alternatives. This adds route-specific pressure information without requiring an LP or adding couplings. Also remove the three-hop Taylor approximation: its pairwise terms can reward partial paths even when the complete three-edge route is absent, creating misleading energy signals.
</DESCRIPTION>
