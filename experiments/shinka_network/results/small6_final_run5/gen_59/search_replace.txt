<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the interaction-count adjustment with a demand-weighted transit-pressure correction. Count each OD demand once, distributing its estimated transit usage across selected intermediates in proportion to their path rewards. Then mildly discount rewards through above-average-use intermediates, with a capped reduction of about 21%.

This targets concentrated transit incentives rather than merely counting couplings. It preserves the sparse coupling graph, direct-link rewards, and structural penalties. The correction is dimensionless and adds no quadratic terms, so it scales naturally across network sizes. Its intended benefit is better load-balancing guidance for both samples and local-search move ranking; evaluation is needed to confirm that benefit.
