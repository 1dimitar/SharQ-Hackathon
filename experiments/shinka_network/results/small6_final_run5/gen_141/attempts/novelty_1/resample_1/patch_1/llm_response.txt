<DESCRIPTION>
Boundedly upweight two-hop route rewards for demands whose source and destination have high aggregate traffic pressure. Those endpoints are likely to need more effective routing capacity, while the existing transit-pressure discount still discourages overloading busy intermediate nodes. The factor is clipped to avoid letting a few high-pressure endpoints dominate, and it adds no couplings or changes to the route-candidate count.
</DESCRIPTION>
