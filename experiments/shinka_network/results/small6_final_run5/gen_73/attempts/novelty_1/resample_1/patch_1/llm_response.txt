<DESCRIPTION>
Blend the reciprocal-link heuristic with an exact two-node cut surrogate, without adding quadratic terms.

For a node pair, let D be its larger incoming/outgoing external demand. With m internal links, its cut has capacity 4−m, giving the congestion lower bound D/(4−m). Subtract the endpoints’ unavoidable singleton bound and retain only positive excess. Its values at m=0,1,2 have an exact quadratic representation in the two reciprocal link variables.

Scale each pair’s contribution by a bounded external-to-incident demand ratio. Blend this correction into 35% of the existing reciprocal penalty, preserving the average reciprocal coefficient. Include the corresponding linear terms, removing endpoint potentials that are constant on valid degree-two networks.

This introduces demand-specific cut information into topology and exchange rankings while retaining most of the successful structural heuristic. Construction remains O(n²), and the number of quadratic terms is unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
