<DESCRIPTION>
Add one tightly capped three-hop alternative only for high-demand endpoint pairs whose best two-hop candidate has weak leg balance. Rank the alternatives using support on all three legs and transit-node pressure, then add small rewards to the two consecutive link pairs. This tests useful longer routes without broadly increasing coupling density or letting the three-hop surrogate overwhelm the existing two-hop model.
</DESCRIPTION>
