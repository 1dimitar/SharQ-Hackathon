<DESCRIPTION>
Make the existing reciprocal-link penalty sensitive to the traffic that must cross the pair’s two-node cut. A reciprocal pair uses two links internally, reducing the links available for external traffic; penalizing it more when external demand is high should improve valid-topology rankings and two-link-exchange guidance. The penalty is moderated by the existing mutual-demand discount, and this changes no coupling keys or term count.
</DESCRIPTION>
