A fixed top-k rule can discard a nearly equally attractive two-hop route, even when the ranking is ambiguous. Keep the existing two-or-three route selection, but add at most one more route when the next candidate’s affinity-to-pressure score is within 8% of the last selected candidate’s score. This provides limited routing flexibility for uncertain OD pairs without broadly increasing the coupling density; the extra route also receives a lower rank factor in the existing scoring loop.
