<DESCRIPTION>
Add a conservative second pass that estimates which transit nodes are likely to carry demand across plausible two-hop routes. The estimate distributes each demand over its three highest-ranked intermediates, then mildly raises the pressure on heavily used nodes before the existing route selection. This makes the route rewards account for aggregate transit load, not just a node’s row and column sums, while preserving the current sparse coupling structure and limiting the adjustment to 18%.
