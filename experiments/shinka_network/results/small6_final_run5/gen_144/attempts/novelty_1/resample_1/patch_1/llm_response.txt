large_n_route_taper
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the influence of sparse two-hop rewards as network size grows. At larger n, each OD pair has more possible intermediates, so rewarding only its top few routes is a less reliable guide and can overstate the value of particular transit links. A slightly steeper taper leaves direct-demand and cut-congestion signals relatively more influential, which may improve energy rankings for the larger instances where the current post-processor has the largest gaps. This changes only route scaling; it leaves the small-instance behavior unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
