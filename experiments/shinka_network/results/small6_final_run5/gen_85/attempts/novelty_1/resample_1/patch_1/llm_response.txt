<DESCRIPTION>
Rank and weight two-hop routes using evidence from both legs rather than letting a strong leg dominate the existing affinity score. The harmonic mean of the legs’ direct-demand support is limited by the weaker leg; a bounded endpoint-share factor modestly adjusts that evidence using each leg’s share of the source’s outgoing or destination’s incoming demand. Applying the same bounded factor to route ranking and coupling magnitude keeps the sparse structure unchanged while making the energy more consistent about which routes it rewards.
</DESCRIPTION>

                # The harmonic mean makes the weaker leg limit route support.
                first_leg = float(support[i, j])
                second_leg = float(support[j, k])
                bilateral = (
                    2.0 * first_leg * second_leg
                    / (first_leg + second_leg + 1e-12)
                )
                source_share = float(z[i, j]) / max(float(outgoing[i]), 1e-12)
                destination_share = float(z[j, k]) / max(float(incoming[k]), 1e-12)
