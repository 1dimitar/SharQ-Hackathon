large_n_route_damping
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Cap the route-size multiplier at 1.0 instead of letting it grow with network size. At n=10 and n=12 this leaves the multiplier unchanged; at n=16 it reduces it from about 1.15 to 1.0. Since the multiplier scales both route rewards and their direct-route redundancy terms, this moderates their influence relative to direct-link and cut terms on larger instances. That may improve sample feasibility and keep the energy ranking useful for the post-processor, where feasibility currently drops most at n=16.
</DESCRIPTION>
