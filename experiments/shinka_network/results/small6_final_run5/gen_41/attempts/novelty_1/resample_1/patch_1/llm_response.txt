<DESCRIPTION>
Add one small three-hop route signal only when the best two-hop route has weak leg affinity, or when the top two two-hop options are similarly ranked and neither is strong. Require positive demand on every three-hop leg, and discount the route by pressure at both transit nodes. The two pairwise couplings provide a sparse QUBO approximation to the three-link path without broad route expansion; keeping the reward modest limits spurious incentives from pairwise terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
