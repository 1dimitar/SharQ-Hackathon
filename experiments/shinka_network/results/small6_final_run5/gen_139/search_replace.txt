<DESCRIPTION>
Rank two-hop candidates partly by the transit node’s pressure relative to the source and destination: a bounded factor favors transit nodes that are less burdened than the endpoints, without allowing pressure to dominate the existing route evidence. Keep two routes by default and add a third only for a high-demand pair whose third candidate is close in rank to the second. This targets route quality while avoiding a broad increase in couplings, which can weaken sample validity and dilute the energy ranking.
