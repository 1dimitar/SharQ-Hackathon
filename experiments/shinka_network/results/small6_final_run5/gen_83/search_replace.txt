<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the raw count of route-reward couplings incident to each link with their total reward magnitude. A link appearing in a few high-demand routes can contribute more to predicted load than one appearing in many weak routes; counting both equally can misidentify which links are overfavoured. The resulting demand-based linear correction targets links that accumulate unusually strong route support, while keeping the existing sparse QUBO structure and coupling count unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
