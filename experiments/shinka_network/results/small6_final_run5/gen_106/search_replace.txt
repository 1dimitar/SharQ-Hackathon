<DESCRIPTION>
Add a compact two-node-cut congestion signal, scaled toward endpoint pairs whose external demand is large relative to their incident demand. The reciprocal-link coupling represents a pair of nodes sharing a small cut; a compensating linear correction keeps the cut signal from simply rewarding or penalizing links uniformly on degree-balanced topologies. The gain is tied to the mean direct-link coefficient so the added signal remains modest and scales with the existing objective. This tests the suggested vulnerability-weighted cut correction without changing the existing route model.
