<DESCRIPTION>
Apply a modest, bounded increase to two-hop route rewards as network size grows, so route information does not weaken relative to direct-link terms at larger n. Also lower the heavy-demand quantile from 0.75 to 0.68, allowing more demands to use the larger candidate set. Both changes are confined to route factors; the scale is capped to avoid letting them dominate the QUBO, and the resulting coupling count remains well below the limit.
</DESCRIPTION>
