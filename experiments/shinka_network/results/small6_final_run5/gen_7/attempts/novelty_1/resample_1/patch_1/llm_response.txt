<DESCRIPTION>
Add a compact reciprocal-link coupling for each unordered node pair. The existing linear terms value each direction separately, but do not express the joint benefit when both directions of a substantial bidirectional demand can be served directly. A small negative coupling, capped by the weaker direction’s normalized demand, rewards that combination without letting a one-way-heavy pair dominate. This adds at most n(n−1)/2 terms and leaves the degree penalty and existing route terms unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>

    # Reward selecting both directions of a pair when both carry demand.
    # Capping by the weaker direction avoids a large one-way flow creating
    # an artificial incentive to add its lightly-used reverse link.
    for i in range(n):
        for j in range(i + 1, n):
            reciprocal_support = min(normalized[i, j], normalized[j, i])
            if reciprocal_support > 0.0:
                key = ((i, j), (j, i))
                quadratic[key] = quadratic.get(key, 0.0) - 0.18 * float(reciprocal_support)
