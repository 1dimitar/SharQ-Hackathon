soften_route_pressure_rank
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the route-ranking pressure exponent from 0.82 to 0.75. The estimated node pressures are only an approximate proxy for transit congestion, so slightly softening their effect should avoid over-discounting useful high-pressure transit nodes and make path selection less brittle. This changes one factor only; the pressure floor and cut-congestion terms remain fixed.
</DESCRIPTION>
