<DESCRIPTION>
Use a softer, power-calibrated relay-pressure discount in the two-hop reward. The current linear discount, `0.60 + 0.80 * pressure`, can strongly favor low-pressure relays over busy ones. Raising a positive-offset pressure score to an exponent below one compresses that difference, while still preferring less-loaded relays. This may reduce overconcentration on a small set of relays and improve the typical sampled topology. The change leaves the direct-link rewards and quadratic-term structure unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
