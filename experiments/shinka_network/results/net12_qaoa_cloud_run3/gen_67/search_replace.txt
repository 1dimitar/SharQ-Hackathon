<DESCRIPTION>
Soften the pressure discount on relay nodes while leaving candidate ranking and selection unchanged. Raising normalized node pressure to 0.75 makes the reward less sensitive to pressure differences, so a relay is not rejected as strongly just because its incident demand is relatively high. This tests the gentler-pressure variant in isolation; the existing edge-pressure discount and all other terms remain intact.
</DESCRIPTION>
