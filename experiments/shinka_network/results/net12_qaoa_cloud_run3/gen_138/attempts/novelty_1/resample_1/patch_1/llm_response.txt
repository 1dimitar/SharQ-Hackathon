softer_relay_pressure
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Use a weaker pressure discount when ranking candidate relays: divide affinity by pressure to the 0.75 power instead of pressure itself. Aggregate node demand is only a rough proxy for transit congestion, so this may avoid excluding useful relays that carry high demand but provide valuable two-hop routes. The path support, relay budget, and quadratic reward formula remain unchanged; only the pressure sensitivity in candidate ranking is adjusted.
</DESCRIPTION>
