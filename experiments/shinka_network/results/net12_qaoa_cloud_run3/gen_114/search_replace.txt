<DESCRIPTION>
Raise the relay-pressure denominator in candidate ranking to the 1.25 power. This makes the ranking more selective against high-pressure transit nodes, favoring relays with more spare capacity when choosing the limited set of two-hop interactions. The existing reward discount and all other term weights remain unchanged, isolating the effect of pressure-aware selection.
</DESCRIPTION>
