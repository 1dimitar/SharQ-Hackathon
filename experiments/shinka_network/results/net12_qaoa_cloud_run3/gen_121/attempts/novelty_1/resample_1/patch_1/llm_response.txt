soften_relay_pressure
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Soften the pressure discount used to rank relay candidates by raising node pressure to 0.75. This gives demand-supported relays with moderate pressure a better chance of being selected, rather than concentrating relay choices on the least-loaded nodes. The direct-link rewards and relay rewards remain unchanged, so this tests pressure sensitivity without changing the reward structure or number of quadratic terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
