adaptive_relay_budget
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Use the recommended baseline relay count `min(n - 2, max(1, n // 5))`, with up to two additional relays for OD demands at or above the 80th-percentile cutoff. This keeps the relay-selection structure intact while reducing unnecessary interactions for smaller instances and reserving extra alternatives for the heaviest demands. The sparser, demand-selective couplings may also make the QUBO easier to partition and solve.
</DESCRIPTION>
