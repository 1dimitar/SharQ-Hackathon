<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the strong-leg contribution in the relay affinity from 0.30 to 0.25. This ranks candidate relays more by the strength of their weaker leg, favoring two-hop routes that are useful on both links rather than routes with one strong and one weak leg. The change preserves the existing pressure adjustment, relay budget, and reward formula.
</DESCRIPTION>
