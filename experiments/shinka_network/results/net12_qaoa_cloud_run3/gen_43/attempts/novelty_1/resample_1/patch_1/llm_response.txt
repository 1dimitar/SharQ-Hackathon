<DESCRIPTION>
Rank each OD pair’s candidate relays by the strength of its weaker leg, with a smaller contribution from the stronger leg. This favors paths where both links can plausibly serve traffic rather than paths with one attractive and one weak link. Retain the existing relay-pressure and edge-overlap discounts, but keep only the best four relay combinations per OD pair. This reduces interaction-graph size and focuses QUBO communities on the most promising two-hop routes, while preserving the existing path-strength and demand-support scales.
</DESCRIPTION>
