<DESCRIPTION>
Use fewer relay candidates for ordinary-demand OD pairs, while retaining extra relay choices for the highest-demand pairs. This follows the promising sparse-construction direction: reducing weak interactions may make communities easier to solve, while the two additional candidates preserve support for the most important traffic. The per-pair budget is capped by the number of valid relay nodes.
</DESCRIPTION>
