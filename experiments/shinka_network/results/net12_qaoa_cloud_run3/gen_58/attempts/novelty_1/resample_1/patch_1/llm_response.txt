<DESCRIPTION>
Rank relay candidates by the usefulness of both legs, using `min(first_leg, second_leg) + 0.25 * max(first_leg, second_leg)`. This favors relays with two serviceable legs over those whose apparent value comes from only one strong leg. The ranking retains the existing relay-pressure and edge-pressure discounts, and leaves the relay budget and path-reward formula unchanged, so the change targets which paths are selected without increasing QUBO density.
