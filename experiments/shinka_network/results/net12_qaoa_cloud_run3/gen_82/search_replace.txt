<DESCRIPTION>
Allocate up to two additional relay candidates only to OD pairs in the top decile of positive demands. This concentrates extra two-hop interactions on the traffic most likely to benefit from alternate routing, while preserving the current relay budget for other pairs. The deterministic 90th-percentile cutoff is computed from the demand matrix, and the number of added quadratic terms remains comfortably below the limit.
</DESCRIPTION>
