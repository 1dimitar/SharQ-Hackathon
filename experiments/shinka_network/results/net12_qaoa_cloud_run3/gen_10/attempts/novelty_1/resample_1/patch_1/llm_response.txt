<DESCRIPTION>
The current penalty weight of 1 is small relative to the many two-hop couplings that can reward a topology with incorrect degrees. The low pre-repair feasible rate (17%) suggests that degree violations are frequently attractive to the QUBO. Increase the penalty with network size, since each link participates in more two-hop interactions as n grows, while keeping it within a moderate range. This should encourage more feasible samples and reduce reliance on repair, without changing the cost terms that distinguish valid topologies.
</DESCRIPTION>
