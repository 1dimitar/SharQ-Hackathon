<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the pressure exponent in relay ranking from 1 to 0.75 while leaving the affinity formula and candidate budgets unchanged. This tests whether the current ranking discounts high-pressure relays too strongly: high-affinity candidates can rank better even when their node pressure is elevated. The selected relay weights and the separate full-path alternative remain unchanged, isolating the effect to candidate ranking.
</DESCRIPTION>
