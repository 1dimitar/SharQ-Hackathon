expand_relay_budget
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the per-OD relay support from roughly one fifth to one third of the nodes, capped at the number of eligible relays. The current budget is only two relays for the 12-node cases, producing a sparse QUBO with 176 quadratic terms. A broader shortlist gives the solver more viable two-hop alternatives when the top-ranked relays are not part of a good overall topology, while still avoiding the broad support of including every relay. This is a targeted test of the suggested support-size sweep; the existing relay ranking and weights remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
