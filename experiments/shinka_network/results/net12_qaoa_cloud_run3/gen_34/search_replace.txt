<DESCRIPTION>
Apply a mild exponent of 0.75 to the pressure discount in both relay ranking and path reward. This tunes pressure avoidance consistently: highly pressured relays are still discounted, but less sharply than with the current linear denominator. That may preserve useful two-hop alternatives and improve solution diversity without changing the sparse interaction structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
