<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the two-hop reward multiplier from 0.46 to 0.36 while leaving the direct-link blend, pressure discounts, and degree penalty unchanged. Two-hop rewards can make a topology look attractive for many individually plausible paths even when those paths compete for the same limited links. A modest reduction should lessen that overcounting and give direct high-demand links more influence, without removing the useful two-hop signal. The intermediate value is a targeted test between the current setting and the weaker two-hop reward that performed less well overall.
</DESCRIPTION>
