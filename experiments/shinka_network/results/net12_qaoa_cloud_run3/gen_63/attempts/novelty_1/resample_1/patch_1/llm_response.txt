<DESCRIPTION>
Rank relay candidates by the usefulness of their weaker leg, using `min(first_leg, second_leg) + 0.25 * max(first_leg, second_leg)`, discounted by relay pressure. This favors paths whose two links can both plausibly support traffic instead of selecting relays mainly because their edge-demand penalty is small. Keep the existing relay budget and pressure/edge-pressure discounts, with a mild rank factor so the top-ranked relay is preferred without dominating the path reward.
