<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the two-hop relay reward coefficient from 0.30 to 0.35 while leaving the support formula, pressure calculation, and relay budget unchanged. This is a targeted test of whether relay support is underweighted relative to direct-link rewards: stronger two-hop interactions may help the QUBO favor topologies that can serve traffic through plausible relays. The change preserves the existing sparsity and coupling structure.
