<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the default relay count to `max(1, n // 5)` and retain up to two extra relays for OD pairs in the top 20% of positive demands. This tests a sparser interaction graph while preserving additional routing options for the most important traffic. The cutoff is computed from normalized demand, and zero-demand matrices safely receive no extra relays.
</DESCRIPTION>
