<DESCRIPTION>
Limit each positive-demand OD pair to its strongest relay candidates instead of coupling every possible two-hop path. Rank relays using the existing pressure-discounted path weight, retaining `max(1, n // 5)` candidates per pair. This produces about 264 quadratic terms at n=12, rather than 880, while preserving the most promising two-hop interactions and keeping the interaction graph sparser for QAOA partitioning.
</DESCRIPTION>
