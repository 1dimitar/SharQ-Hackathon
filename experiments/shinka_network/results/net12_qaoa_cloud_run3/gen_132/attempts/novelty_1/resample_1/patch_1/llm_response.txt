<DESCRIPTION>
Rank relay candidates by a bottleneck-aware affinity instead of the sum of their two leg demands. The current sum can favor a relay with one strong leg and one nearly unusable leg; a minimum-plus-fractional-maximum score still values a strong leg but makes the weaker leg matter more. This affects which relays enter the sparse interaction set while leaving the existing pressure discount, reward construction, and term budget unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
