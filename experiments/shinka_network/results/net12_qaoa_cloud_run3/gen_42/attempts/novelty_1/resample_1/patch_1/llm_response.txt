<DESCRIPTION>
Rank candidate relays by requiring both legs to contribute, while giving a useful stronger leg some additional credit: change the affinity to `min(a, b) + 0.25 * max(a, b)`. This increases the stronger-leg contribution from 0.15 to 0.25 without allowing it to dominate a weak leg. The existing pressure discount, relay budget, and reward weights remain unchanged.
