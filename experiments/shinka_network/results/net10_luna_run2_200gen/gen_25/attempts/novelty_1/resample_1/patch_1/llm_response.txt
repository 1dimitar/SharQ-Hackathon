<DESCRIPTION>
For each two-hop path, retain the current discount for edge pressure, but multiply its reward by a bounded support factor derived from the normalized direct demands on both route edges. The nonzero floor keeps potentially useful paths available even when one edge has little direct demand; the increasing factor favors paths whose component links are already supported by traffic. This changes weights only for the existing two-hop terms and adds no quadratic terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
