<DESCRIPTION>
For each positive-demand OD pair, the existing loop can add a reward for every possible two-hop route. Together, those alternatives may outweigh the direct-link reward even when no individual route is compelling. Collect the pair’s two-hop terms and proportionally scale them only when their total magnitude exceeds 0.75 of that pair’s direct-link reward. This preserves the relative ranking of candidate intermediate nodes while limiting aggregate two-hop support. The quadratic term count is unchanged.
