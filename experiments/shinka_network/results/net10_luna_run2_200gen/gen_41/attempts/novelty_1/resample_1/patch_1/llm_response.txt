<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the edge-pressure coefficient from 0.5 to 0.75 while leaving the direct-link rewards, transit-node discount, and path strength unchanged. This tests whether more strongly discouraging two-hop paths that use links with substantial direct demand improves load balance, without changing the rest of the surrogate. The edit adds no quadratic terms and is inexpensive to evaluate.
</DESCRIPTION>
