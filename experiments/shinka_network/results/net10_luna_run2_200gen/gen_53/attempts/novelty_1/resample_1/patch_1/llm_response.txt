<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the two-hop reward budget from 0.25 to 0.62 while leaving the existing path support and pressure discounts unchanged. This tests whether the current QUBO underweights indirect service relative to direct-link rewards: with only two outgoing links per node, useful two-hop routes can be important alternatives to direct links. Keeping the support allocation fixed isolates the effect of reward strength and does not increase the number of quadratic terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
