<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the discount on two-hop rewards for paths whose component edges carry substantial direct demand, using a power of 1.5 rather than a linear denominator. This tests stronger, nonlinear edge-load discounting while leaving the direct-link rewards, node pressure, and path support unchanged. It preserves the current term count and may steer the low-energy region away from concentrating traffic on already-demand-heavy edges.
</DESCRIPTION>
