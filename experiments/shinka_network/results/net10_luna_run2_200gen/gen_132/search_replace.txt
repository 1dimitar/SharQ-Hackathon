<DESCRIPTION>
Narrow the relative-quality range for two-hop rewards. With the current support formula, low-demand OD pairs receive a larger relative bonus than high-demand pairs; capping that bonus more tightly should keep the indirect terms focused on substantial traffic rather than letting many weak paths shape the typical sampled topology. This preserves the direct-demand signal and the existing term count, while testing a more conservative route-quality bound.
</DESCRIPTION>
