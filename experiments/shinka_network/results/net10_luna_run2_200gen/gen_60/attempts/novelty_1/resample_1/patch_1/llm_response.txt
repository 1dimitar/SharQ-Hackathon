<DESCRIPTION>
Raise the evaluator’s degree-penalty weight slightly from 1.0 to 1.05. The current samples are already almost always feasible, so this is a deliberately small adjustment: it may discourage near-feasible low-energy samples that need repair, without substantially changing the demand-based reward structure or suppressing topology exploration. Since the evaluator rescales the objective terms before applying this penalty, the change is local and bounded; compare feasibility and both best and median gaps to ensure the stronger penalty does not harm topology quality.
