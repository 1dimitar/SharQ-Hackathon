<DESCRIPTION>
Keep the two-hop path coefficient fixed at 0.12, but discount its reward when either route edge already has substantial direct demand. This separates the path reward from the edge-pressure adjustment: a two-hop combination is less attractive when it may add load to links that are already valuable for direct traffic. The 0.25 discount strength is a conservative test of the suggested lower setting, and the change preserves the existing quadratic-term count.
</DESCRIPTION>
