<DESCRIPTION>
Estimate relay pressure from the larger of a node’s incoming and outgoing demand totals, rather than their sum. Transit requires both an incoming and an outgoing link, and each node has only two of each; a node with a strongly one-sided traffic profile can therefore be a bottleneck even when its combined incident total looks moderate. Using the larger directional total in the existing path discount is a targeted way to discourage over-reliance on such relays while leaving direct-link rewards and the number of quadratic terms unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
