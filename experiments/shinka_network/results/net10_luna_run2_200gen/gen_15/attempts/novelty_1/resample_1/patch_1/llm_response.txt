<DESCRIPTION>
Add a modest endpoint-activity signal to the direct-demand reward, using a 90/10 mix. Nodes with high total outgoing or incoming traffic are likely to need useful links even when a particular OD pair has modest direct demand. Keeping direct demand dominant makes this a conservative adjustment; the existing two-hop terms remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
