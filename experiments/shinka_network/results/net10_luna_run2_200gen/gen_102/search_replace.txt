<DESCRIPTION>
Apply a mild concave transform to normalized through-demand when setting the total two-hop reward. This lets smaller positive OD demands contribute more relative to the largest demands, while retaining demand-local path selection and the existing cap on indirect reward. Direct-link rewards and candidate-path ranking remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
