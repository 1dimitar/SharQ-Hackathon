<DESCRIPTION>
Use a mild square-root transform for direct-demand rewards while retaining most of their linear signal. This gives moderate-demand direct links more influence, which may help the typical sampled topology, while preserving the preference for links serving the largest demands. The change is limited to the direct-demand component; endpoint and two-hop terms remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
