<DESCRIPTION>
For each OD pair, the current code can reward up to \(n-2\) alternative two-hop paths independently. Their combined influence may overwhelm the direct-link preference even when each individual path reward is modest. Accumulate the candidate two-hop rewards for that OD pair, then proportionally scale them when their total magnitude exceeds 0.75 times the direct-link reward. This retains the relative ranking of relay choices while limiting their aggregate influence.
