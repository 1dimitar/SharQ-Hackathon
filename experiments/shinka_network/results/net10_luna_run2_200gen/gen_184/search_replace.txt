<DESCRIPTION>
Give moderate-demand direct links more influence by blending normalized demand with its square root. This narrows the gap between high- and moderate-demand link rewards, which may improve the quality of typical sampled topologies while retaining a substantial reward for directly serving heavy traffic. The existing two-hop terms remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
