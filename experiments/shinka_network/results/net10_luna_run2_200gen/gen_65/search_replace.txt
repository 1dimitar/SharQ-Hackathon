<DESCRIPTION>
Discount two-hop alternatives that use links with substantial direct demand. Those links are more likely to be busy already, so reallocating the fixed indirect-reward budget toward less-loaded alternatives better reflects the congestion the routing objective measures. Also raise the evaluator-supplied degree penalty slightly from 1.0 to 1.1 to discourage the small fraction of infeasible pre-repair samples without making a large change to the cost balance.
</DESCRIPTION>
