# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns
- **The current best, Generation 14 (“stronger_two_hop”), remains the strongest overall result:** score **0.82**, mean best gap **10.53%**, median gap **35.22%**, and **99% feasibility**. Its winning combination is simple normalized direct-demand rewards plus selective, supported two-hop rewards discounted for transit-node and route-edge pressure.
- **Pressure-aware, selective path rewards outperform the newer demand-mixture variants.** Generation 14’s mean best gap is lower than Generation 29 (**13.70%**), Generation 30 (**12.75%**), Generation 32 (**15.05%**), Generation 33 (**12.86%**), and Generation 37 (**12.48%**); all five score **0.80–0.81**, versus 0.82 for the current best.
- **Generation 22 (“edge_pressure_discount”) remains a close alternative on typical samples:** its median gap is **34.81%**, slightly below Generation 14’s **35.22%**, but its mean best gap is worse (**11.98% vs. 10.53%**). This reinforces that the current best balances best-sample quality and typical-sample quality well.

## Ineffective Approaches
- **Demand-shape mixtures continue to underperform the current best.** Generation 29’s direct-demand, square-root, and endpoint-traffic incentives yield a **13.70%** mean best gap; Generation 30’s 90/10 linear–square-root blend yields **12.75%**. Both have **0.81** scores and median gaps around **36%**, compared with Generation 14’s **10.53%** best gap and **35.22%** median.
- **More elaborate transit adjustments did not reliably improve results.** Generation 32 uses shared two-hop rewards adjusted for busy transit nodes and has the weakest new mean best gap (**15.05%**, score **0.80**). Generation 33 blends two transit-pressure schemes and achieves **12.86%**; Generation 37 discounts by transit load and divides rewards across four paths, achieving **12.48%**. Each remains behind Generation 14’s more selective pressure-discounted path formulation.
- **High feasibility and topology diversity do not explain the quality gap.** Generations 29, 30, 32, 33, and 37 all report 64 distinct topologies and 100% mean feasibility, yet their median gaps range from **35.24% to 38.31%** and best gaps from **12.48% to 15.05%**.
