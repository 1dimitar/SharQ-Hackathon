<DESCRIPTION>
Apply a mild concave transform only to the two-hop through-demand signal. The linear direct-link rewards remain unchanged, preserving their useful demand ranking, while the square-root component gives medium-sized through-demands more influence relative to the largest demands. This may help the sampled topologies balance indirect routing opportunities instead of focusing too heavily on only the biggest OD pairs.
</DESCRIPTION>
