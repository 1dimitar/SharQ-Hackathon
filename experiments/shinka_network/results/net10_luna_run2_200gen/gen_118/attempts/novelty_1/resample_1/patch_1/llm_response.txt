<DESCRIPTION>
Raise the two-hop reward strength from 0.25 to 0.46. The current surrogate already rewards only positive-demand two-hop routes and discounts pressured intermediates, but its route reward may be too weak to influence topology selection relative to direct-link rewards. This targeted increase follows the suggested strength sweep while staying below 0.56, which may help sampled topologies serve more traffic through useful routes without making the two-hop terms dominant. It does not change the term count or degree-penalty handling.
