Increase the edge-pressure discount from 0.5 to 0.85 while keeping the existing relay-pressure and path logic fixed. This more strongly reduces the reward for two-hop paths that use edges associated with high demand, which may help avoid concentrating traffic on already pressured links. It is a targeted change that preserves the current number of quadratic terms and tests the suggested adjustment directly.
