The current two-hop rewards are strong enough to reduce the fraction of samples that already satisfy the degree constraints (82%, versus 98–99% for earlier, milder settings). Reduce their overall strength so they are less likely to overwhelm the evaluator’s degree penalty. Within that smaller contribution, use a mild concave transform of through-demand: it preserves the preference for useful high-volume paths while allowing moderate-demand paths to influence the topology. This is a targeted test of the suggested demand-dependent adjustment, with the lower strength intended to retain feasible samples.
