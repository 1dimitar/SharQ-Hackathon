<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the two-hop reward budget from 0.8 to 0.62 while leaving the direct-link rewards and transit-pressure formula unchanged. Since a two-hop demand can receive credit from several candidate paths, a more moderate reward should limit overcounting and keep the QUBO’s low-energy topologies more strongly guided by direct demand. This tests a lower path-strength setting without changing the term structure or term count.
</DESCRIPTION>
