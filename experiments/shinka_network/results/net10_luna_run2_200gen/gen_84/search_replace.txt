<DESCRIPTION>
The current quadratic loop gives every candidate two-hop path an independent reward, so an OD pair with many possible intermediates can accumulate a large reward and dominate the direct-link signal. This edit caps the total indirect reward for each OD pair at a modest fraction of that pair’s demand-based signal, then allocates that budget among paths according to component-link support and transit pressure. It retains path-specific guidance while preventing aggregate two-hop incentives from overwhelming direct demand.
</DESCRIPTION>
