</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Change only the relay-pressure normalization, keeping route scoring and all other terms fixed. Normalize incident demand by its maximum rather than its mean, and use a 0.55 floor. This makes the pressure scale consistent across demand matrices and gives quieter nodes a measured preference as relays without allowing extremely low-demand nodes to dominate route selection. Comparing this isolated change against the current mean-based, 0.75-floor version will show whether the altered relay preference helps both the best and typical sampled topologies.
</DESCRIPTION>
