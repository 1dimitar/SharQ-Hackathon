<DESCRIPTION>
The current two-hop allocation favors paths whose component links already carry substantial direct demand through the geometric-support term, but it does not explicitly suppress those potentially congested links. Discount each intermediate’s evidence by the average normalized demand on its two component edges. Using a moderate coefficient of 0.5 should temper this effect without eliminating useful demand-supported paths, and it changes only the allocation of the existing OD-pair budget.
