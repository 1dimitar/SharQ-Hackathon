max_normalized_transit_pressure
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Normalize transit-node incident demand by the maximum rather than the mean, and use a bounded discount from 1.0 to 0.75. This avoids making low-load nodes disproportionately attractive—as the current mean-based inverse factor can—while still mildly discouraging routes through the busiest nodes. The change preserves the existing two-hop terms and their count.
</DESCRIPTION>
