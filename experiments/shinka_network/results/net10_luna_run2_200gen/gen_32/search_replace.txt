<DESCRIPTION>
Use maximum incident traffic rather than mean incident traffic to normalize transit-node pressure, and floor the inverse-pressure factor at 0.75. This makes the discount less sensitive to the average node load and limits how strongly busy nodes lose two-hop-path reward. It tests the recommended pressure-normalization change while keeping the existing demand-based path rewards and linear terms unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
