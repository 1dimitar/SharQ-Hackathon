<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the two-hop reward strength from 0.52 to 0.40. Two-hop terms help represent indirect service, but rewarding many possible paths can make the typical sampled topology less focused on high-value direct links. A more moderate strength tests whether preserving the direct-link signal improves the median topology while retaining useful two-hop guidance. This is a controlled single-parameter change; compare it against the current 0.52 setting.
</DESCRIPTION>
