<DESCRIPTION>
The current two-hop reward is assigned independently across every possible intermediate, so its aggregate influence for an OD pair can overwhelm the direct-link signal. Collect the candidate two-hop weights per OD pair, then proportionally scale them when their total magnitude exceeds 0.75 of that pair’s direct-link reward. This preserves the relative preference among intermediates while restraining indirect-route rewards that might otherwise distort topology choices.
