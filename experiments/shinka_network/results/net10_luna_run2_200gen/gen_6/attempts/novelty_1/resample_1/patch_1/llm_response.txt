<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the generic two-hop reward with positive pairwise congestion costs for links that share a source or destination. A node has only two outgoing and two incoming links, so selecting two links that each carry substantial direct demand can concentrate load at that endpoint. The geometric-mean weights penalize such pairs while remaining bounded by the demand scale. This keeps the existing direct-demand signal and uses at most \(n(n-1)(n-2)\) quadratic terms, within the limit.
</DESCRIPTION>
