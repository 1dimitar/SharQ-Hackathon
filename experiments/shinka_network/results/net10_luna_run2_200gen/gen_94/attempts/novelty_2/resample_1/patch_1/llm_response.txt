<DESCRIPTION>
Discount two-hop rewards when either component link already has substantial direct demand. The current transit-node discount does not account for pressure on the links themselves; adding an edge-pressure factor makes paths using busy links less attractive. This tests the stronger `1 + 0.75 * edge_pressure` suppression while leaving the other terms unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
