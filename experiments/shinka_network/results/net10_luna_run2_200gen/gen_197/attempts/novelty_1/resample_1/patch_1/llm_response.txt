<DESCRIPTION>
Estimate relay pressure from the average of a node’s incoming and outgoing demand, rather than letting either side dominate. Keep the existing mean normalization and raise the pressure floor to 0.65, so low-demand nodes are not treated as nearly uncongested transit points. This changes only the relay discount used by the existing two-hop model; it does not add quadratic terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
