<DESCRIPTION>
Normalize relay pressure by mean incident demand rather than the maximum, with a 0.75 floor. Maximum-based normalization compresses pressure differences because the busiest node is fixed at 1 while many other nodes hit the floor. Mean normalization makes the discount reflect how busy a relay is relative to the network overall; the higher floor limits excessive preference for quiet nodes. This changes only the route discount, leaving the existing path-quality formula and term count intact.
</DESCRIPTION>
