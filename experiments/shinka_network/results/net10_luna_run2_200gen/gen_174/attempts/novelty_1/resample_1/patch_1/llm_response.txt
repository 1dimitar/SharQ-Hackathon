<DESCRIPTION>
Shift the direct-link reward from 0.85/0.15 to 0.75/0.25 between normalized demand and its square root. This controlled adjustment gives moderate-demand links more influence, which may improve typical sampled topologies while retaining a substantial preference for high-demand direct links. The two-hop terms and all other settings remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
