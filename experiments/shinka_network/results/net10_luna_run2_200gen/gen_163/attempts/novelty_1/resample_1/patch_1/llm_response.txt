<DESCRIPTION>
Increase the two-hop path reward from 0.46 to 0.56 while leaving the demand-dependent path support and pressure discount unchanged. Direct-link rewards remain in place, but stronger support for useful two-hop routes should help the QUBO represent traffic that cannot be served directly under the degree constraints. This is a targeted test supported by prior results in which a 0.56 path strength outperformed 0.46, though the surrounding path formula differed.
</DESCRIPTION>
