<DESCRIPTION>
The current transit discount ranges from 1.0 for the least-loaded node down to 0.5 for the busiest, which may discourage useful intermediates too strongly. Keep the existing maximum-incident normalization, support allocation, and indirect budget unchanged, but use a gentler capacity discount with a floor of 0.85. This tests whether preserving more incentive for busy transit nodes improves the low-energy topologies, particularly on the weak network10_s2 case.
</DESCRIPTION>
