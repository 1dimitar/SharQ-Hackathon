<DESCRIPTION>
Calibrate the existing incident-demand transit penalty using a pressure floor of 0.75, with pressure measured relative to mean incident demand. Using the inverse square root of floored pressure gives low-incident nodes a modest preference as intermediates while discounting high-pressure nodes. This increases the relative distinction between transit candidates without making the penalty as sharp as a direct inverse-pressure rule.
</DESCRIPTION>
