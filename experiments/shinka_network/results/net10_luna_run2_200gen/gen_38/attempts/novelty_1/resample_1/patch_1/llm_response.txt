bounded_path_support
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Scale each eligible two-hop reward by a bounded measure of support from its two component edges. Paths whose edges both carry substantial direct demand receive a modestly stronger incentive, while the positive multiplier floor preserves the existing incentive for paths with lightly loaded edges. The current positive-through-demand gate and transit-pressure discount remain unchanged, and this adds no quadratic terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
