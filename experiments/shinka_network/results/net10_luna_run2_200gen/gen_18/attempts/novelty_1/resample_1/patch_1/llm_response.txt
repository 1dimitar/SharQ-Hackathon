<DESCRIPTION>
Gate each two-hop reward by how plausible its two component links are, using the geometric mean of their normalized direct demands. Retain a 25% floor so a useful transit link is not discarded merely because it carries little direct demand. This keeps the existing OD-demand routing signal while reducing rewards for paths whose component links have little demand support; the number of quadratic terms is unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
