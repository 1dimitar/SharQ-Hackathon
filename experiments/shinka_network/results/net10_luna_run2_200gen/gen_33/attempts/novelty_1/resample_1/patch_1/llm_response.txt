edge_pressure_discount
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Discount indirect-path rewards when either constituent edge already has substantial direct demand. This is a focused test of edge-aware routing pressure: paths using heavily demanded links are less attractive as alternatives, while low-pressure links retain more incentive to support transit. Use a conservative coefficient of 0.25 so the adjustment refines, rather than overwhelms, the existing transit-node discount.
</DESCRIPTION>
