<DESCRIPTION>
Switch relay-pressure normalization from mean incident demand to maximum incident demand, while keeping the existing 0.75 floor and all route-reward settings unchanged. This isolates the normalization choice: most nodes will receive the floor pressure, while the busiest nodes are discounted relative to the single most loaded node. The softer, more concentrated pressure adjustment may avoid over-penalizing useful relays and improve the typical sampled topology.
</DESCRIPTION>
