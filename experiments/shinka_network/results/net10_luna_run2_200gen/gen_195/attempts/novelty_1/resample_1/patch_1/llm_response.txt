<DESCRIPTION>
Raise the relay-pressure floor from 0.5 to 0.65 and express pressure using the average of incoming and outgoing demand. This avoids strongly discounting a node that is busy in only one direction, while preserving the existing mean-based normalization. The higher floor also limits how much otherwise useful relays are penalized, which may improve the typical sampled topology.
</DESCRIPTION>
