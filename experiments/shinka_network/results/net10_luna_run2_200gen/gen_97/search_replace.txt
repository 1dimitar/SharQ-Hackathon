<DESCRIPTION>
Increase suppression of two-hop rewards when either link already carries substantial direct demand. The current denominator, `1 + 0.5 * edge_pressure`, discounts those routes only mildly; replacing it with `(1 + edge_pressure) ** 1.5` makes routes through potentially loaded links less attractive. This tests the suggested stronger-suppression direction while leaving direct-link rewards, node pressure, and all other terms unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
