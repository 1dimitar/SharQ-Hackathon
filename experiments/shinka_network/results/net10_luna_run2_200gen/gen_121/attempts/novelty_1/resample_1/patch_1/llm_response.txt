<DESCRIPTION>
Keep the route-quality, congestion, and indirect-budget settings unchanged, but apply a 0.75 floor to the existing max-normalized relay pressure. This isolates the effect of pressure normalization: very quiet nodes no longer receive a disproportionate transit reward solely because their incident demand is small, while busier nodes retain their relative pressure. The floor is a focused test of the suggested max-based normalization variants and avoids changing unrelated route-scoring behavior.
</DESCRIPTION>
