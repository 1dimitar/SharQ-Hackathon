<DESCRIPTION>
Keep the positive-demand gate for two-hop paths, but modulate each path reward by a bounded support multiplier based on the normalized demands of both constituent links. The multiplier has a nonzero floor, so a useful path is never suppressed, while paths whose edges already have stronger direct-demand support receive a modestly larger reward. This adds a selective signal without letting the multiplier overwhelm the existing traffic and transit-congestion terms.
</DESCRIPTION>
