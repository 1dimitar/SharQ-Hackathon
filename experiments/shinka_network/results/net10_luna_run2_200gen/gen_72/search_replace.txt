<DESCRIPTION>
Discount two-hop rewards according to the transit node’s incident demand, normalized by the busiest node rather than by an average. A modest 0.85 floor prevents the busiest node from being penalized so heavily that useful routes through it lose all incentive. This adds no quadratic terms and retains the existing direct-demand and edge-pressure signals.
